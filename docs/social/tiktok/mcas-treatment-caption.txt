MCAS: treatment options 🛡️

💊 Non-drowsy antihistamines such as cetirizine, loratadine or fexofenadine are usually tried first. Only take a higher dose if your doctor says so
🫄 H2 blockers such as famotidine can help with gut symptoms like tummy pain and feeling sick
🧪 Other options include sodium cromoglicate, montelukast and short courses of steroids
📓 A symptom diary can show your triggers. Ask about a dietitian before cutting out foods
⚠️ Some painkillers and anaesthetics can trigger symptoms. Tell every doctor, dentist and pharmacist you have MCAS
