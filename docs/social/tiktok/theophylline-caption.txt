Theophylline: what to know 💊

🫁 It opens the airways and is an add-on treatment for asthma and COPD when inhalers are not enough
⚖️ The gap between a helpful and a harmful dose is small, so you need blood tests to check the level
🚬 Smoking, illness, some antibiotics and many other medicines can change the level
🔁 Stay on the same brand, and tell your doctor and pharmacist about any changes
🚨 Signs of too much: being sick a lot, a very fast or irregular heartbeat, or fits. Get help straight away
