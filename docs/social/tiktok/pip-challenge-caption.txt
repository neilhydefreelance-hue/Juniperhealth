PIP decision wrong? Challenge it 💪

⏰ Ask for a mandatory reconsideration within one month of the date on your decision letter
📞 Ask by phone, by letter or on form CRMR1. If you phone, follow it up in writing and keep a copy
📝 Say which activities you disagree with, why (with examples), what the assessment report got wrong, and send any new evidence
⚖️ Still disagree? Appeal to an independent tribunal within one month of your reconsideration notice. It is not part of the DWP
✅ Most PIP appeals that reach a hearing are decided in the claimant's favour
