Levetiracetam: epilepsy medicine explained 💊

🧠 One of the most widely used epilepsy medicines, treating several types of seizure
😴 Common side effects: feeling sleepy or tired, headache, dizziness, irritability, and low mood or anxiety
👀 Tell your doctor if you or your family notice changes in your mood or behaviour, such as aggression or depression
🛑 Do not stop suddenly, as it can cause seizures. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly over weeks
🚗 Follow DVLA rules on driving with epilepsy
