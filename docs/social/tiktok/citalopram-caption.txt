Citalopram: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats depression and panic disorder
⏳ It usually takes 4 to 6 weeks to work fully. Some people feel more anxious at first
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, headache, sweating, dry mouth and sleep changes
💓 It can affect your heart rhythm at higher doses. Do not take it with St John's wort
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly

General information, not medical advice. Tell your doctor straight away if you feel worse or have thoughts of suicide, especially if you are under 25. In an emergency call 999. Samaritans: 116 123.
