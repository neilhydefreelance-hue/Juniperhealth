Need help right now? Save this 💜

🚨 In immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. If you cannot speak, call 999 and press 55 when asked
🏥 Mental health crisis: call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option, in England and Wales
📞 Samaritans: call free on 116 123, day or night
💬 Shout: text SHOUT to 85258 for free, 24 hour text support
🏴 CALL helpline in Wales: call free on 0800 132 737, or text "help" to 81066

More free help on our Get help now page: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

You are not alone. These services are free and confidential.
