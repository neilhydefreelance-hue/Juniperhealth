Sertraline: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats depression, panic and social anxiety, OCD and PTSD
⏳ It usually takes 4 to 6 weeks to work fully. Some people feel more anxious at first
🤢 Common side effects include feeling sick, headache, sleep changes, dizziness and sweating
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly
🆘 Tell your doctor straight away if you feel worse or have thoughts of suicide
