Aids that help with asthma 🫁

💊 On prescription: a spacer, a peak flow meter and an asthma action plan
🧼 Wash your spacer about once a month in warm soapy water, let it drip dry, and replace it every year
🛏️ Cut triggers: wash bedding at 60°C, reduce damp and mould, wear a scarf in cold air and sunglasses on high pollen days
🏠 Renting with damp or mould? Your landlord has a legal duty to deal with it
🚑 Call 999 if your reliever is not helping, you are too breathless to speak, or your lips look blue or grey
