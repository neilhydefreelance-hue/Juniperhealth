Free cinema ticket for your carer 🎬

🎟️ With a CEA Card, buy a full price ticket and get one free for someone to go with you and help you
✅ You can usually apply if you get PIP (either part), DLA (any rate), Attendance Allowance or Armed Forces Independence Payment
👁️ You can also apply if you are registered blind or sight impaired
💷 It costs £6. Apply online at the CEA Card website and upload your proof
🍿 Many cinemas run autism-friendly and subtitled showings. Check your local cinema

Read about the CEA Card: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Accepted at most UK cinemas.
