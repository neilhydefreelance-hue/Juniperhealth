Bipolar explained, part 2 of 3: what is bipolar 2? 💜

🌤️ Bipolar 2 means episodes of hypomania and depression, but never full mania
📅 Hypomania is a milder high that lasts at least 4 days
⚠️ It is not "milder bipolar". Depression in bipolar 2 is often long and severe
🩺 Many people are first diagnosed with depression. If you have ever had times of unusually high mood or energy, tell your GP
💊 Treatment can include lithium, lamotrigine, quetiapine, talking therapies and steady routines
