PTSD: grounding tips for flashbacks 💜

🖐️ Try 5, 4, 3, 2, 1: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste
🦶 Press your feet into the floor, or hold something cold, like an ice cube
🗣️ Remind yourself: "That was then. This is now. I am safe."
🌙 Keep a calming bedtime routine. Tell your GP or therapist if nightmares are frequent
🍷 Alcohol and drugs tend to make PTSD worse over time. Your GP can help

General information, not medical advice. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123, or 999 in an emergency.
