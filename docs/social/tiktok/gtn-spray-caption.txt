GTN spray for angina ❤️

💨 A spray or tablet under the tongue widens blood vessels and relieves angina chest pain within minutes
🪑 Sit down before using it, as it can make you feel faint
🚑 Call 999 if chest pain has not gone 5 minutes after the second dose
🚫 Never take it with medicines for erection problems such as sildenafil. Blood pressure can fall dangerously
🤕 Common side effects: headache, flushing and feeling dizzy when standing up
