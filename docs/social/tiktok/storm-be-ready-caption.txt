Disabled? Be ready for storm season ⛈️

📋 The government's new Be Ready campaign asks every household to be ready to manage at home for 3 days
💧 The checklist: 3 litres of water per person per day, food that needs no cooking, a torch, radio, power bank, cash and a written list of your medicines
⚡ Join the free Priority Services Register with your energy supplier, your electricity network operator and your water company
🔋 Rely on equipment? Tell your network operator, ask about battery life, and charge your wheelchair when a storm is forecast
🤝 Agree a plan with someone you trust to come round if the power goes off and you cannot call

Read our storm guide: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Power cut: call 105. Emergency: 999. NHS 111. Samaritans: 116 123. Never use a barbecue or generator indoors.
