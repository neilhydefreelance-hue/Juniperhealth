Pay no VAT on disability products 💜

💷 If you have a long-term illness or disability, you can buy many disability products without VAT
✅ You do not need to get a benefit to qualify
🛋️ Things like riser recliner chairs, stairlifts, mobility scooters, incontinence products and bathroom adaptations can qualify
📝 Tell the seller you are eligible and fill in a short declaration
🤝 British Red Cross loans, charity grants and Access to Work can help too

Read our free home and equipment guide: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Ordinary items like a standard bed do not qualify.
