Glaucoma: the silent sight thief 💜

👁️ Glaucoma damages the optic nerve that connects your eye to your brain
📊 Around 700,000 people in the UK have it, and many do not know
🤫 The most common type has no symptoms at first and slowly affects the edges of your sight
⚠️ You are more at risk if you are over 40, have a close relative with it, are of African, Caribbean or Asian origin, have diabetes or are very short-sighted
👓 Regular eye tests can catch it early. Drops, laser or surgery can protect your sight
🚨 Go to A&E for a sudden very painful red eye, blurred sight or halos
