What is eczema? Explained simply 💜

🧴 Eczema makes skin itchy, dry, cracked and sore
📊 It affects around 1 in 5 children and 1 in 10 adults in the UK
📍 It often appears on the hands, inside elbows, backs of knees, and the face and scalp in children
🧼 Triggers include soaps, heat and sweat, cold dry weather, wool, stress, dust mites and pets
💧 Daily emollients, steroid creams, wet wraps, light therapy and specialist medicines can help
⚠️ Weepy, crusty or very painful eczema may be infected. Get help the same day
