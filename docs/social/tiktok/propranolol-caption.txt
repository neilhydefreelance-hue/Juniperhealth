Propranolol: what to know 💊

💓 A beta blocker used to prevent migraines, ease physical signs of anxiety, and treat high blood pressure, irregular heartbeat and tremor
🛑 It blocks adrenaline, slowing your heart rate and easing shaking and a pounding heart
😴 Common side effects: tiredness, cold hands and feet, sleep problems or nightmares, and dizziness
⚠️ Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly
🫁 Not suitable for most people with asthma

General information, not medical advice. Propranolol is very dangerous in overdose. If you have thoughts of self-harm, tell your doctor, or call Samaritans free on 116 123. In an emergency call 999.
