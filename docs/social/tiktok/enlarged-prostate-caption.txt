Enlarged prostate explained 💜

👨 An enlarged prostate is very common in men and people with a prostate over 50
🚽 Signs include difficulty starting to pee, a weak flow, peeing more often, getting up at night and dribbling
🩺 Your GP may suggest a bladder diary, a urine test, an examination and a PSA blood test
💊 Bladder training, tamsulosin, finasteride and procedures like TURP can help
🚨 Go to A&E if you cannot pee at all, especially with a painful, swollen tummy
