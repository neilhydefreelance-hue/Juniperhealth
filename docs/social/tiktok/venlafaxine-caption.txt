Venlafaxine: what to know 💊

🧠 An SNRI antidepressant for depression, generalised anxiety, social anxiety and panic disorder
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, headache, sweating, dizziness and trouble sleeping
⏰ Try not to miss doses. Withdrawal can start within hours, with dizziness and electric shock feelings
📉 Your doctor will lower the dose very slowly if you stop. It can also raise blood pressure
🆘 Get help straight away for thoughts of harming yourself, chest pain or a severe headache
