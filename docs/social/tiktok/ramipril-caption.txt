Ramipril: what to know 💊

❤️ An ACE inhibitor for high blood pressure, heart failure, after a heart attack and for kidney disease
😷 A dry, tickly cough is a common side effect. Tell your doctor if it bothers you
🤒 Sick day rules: if you are being sick or have diarrhoea and cannot drink, you may need to pause it. Ask your doctor
🩸 You need regular blood tests. Avoid salt substitutes that contain potassium. Not for use in pregnancy
🚑 Call 999 if your face, lips, tongue or throat swell
