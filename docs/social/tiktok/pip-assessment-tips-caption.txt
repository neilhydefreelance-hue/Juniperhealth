PIP assessment coming up? How to prepare 📋

📖 Before the day: re-read your form, write notes on each activity, list your medicines, and bring someone with you
🎙️ You can ask for your assessment to be recorded. Ask as soon as you get your appointment letter
🗓️ Be honest and specific. Describe how things are on most days, not a good day
😓 Explain the pain, tiredness and how long it takes you to recover after doing things
👀 Assessors notice small things, like how you walked in or managed a coat. If it hurt, say so at the time
