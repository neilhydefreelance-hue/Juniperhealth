Carbamazepine (Tegretol): what to know 💊

🧠 It treats epilepsy, trigeminal neuralgia and other nerve pain, and is sometimes used for bipolar disorder
😵 Common side effects: feeling dizzy or unsteady, double vision, feeling sick and low sodium
🚨 A skin rash, especially with blisters, peeling, mouth ulcers or a high temperature, needs help straight away
🤰 It can harm an unborn baby and makes the pill less effective. Talk to your doctor
🛑 Do not stop suddenly, as it can trigger seizures. It also interacts with many medicines
