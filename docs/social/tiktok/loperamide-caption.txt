Loperamide (Imodium): what to know 💊

🚽 It slows down the bowel so poo is firmer and less frequent, and can help long-term diarrhoea in IBS
⚠️ Do not use it if there is blood in your poo, you have a high temperature, or during a colitis flare, unless a doctor advises
🛑 Never take more than the recommended dose. Very high doses can cause serious heart problems
🤢 Common side effects: constipation, wind, stomach cramps and feeling sick
🩺 Get help for a swollen, painful tummy, especially with a high temperature
