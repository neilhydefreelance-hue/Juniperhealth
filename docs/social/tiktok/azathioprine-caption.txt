Azathioprine: what to know 💊

🛡️ It calms down the immune system, for Crohn's and colitis, rheumatoid arthritis, after a transplant, and other autoimmune conditions
🩸 You need a blood test before starting, and regular blood tests during treatment
☀️ It increases the risk of skin cancer. Use sunscreen and check your skin
⚠️ A high temperature, sore throat, mouth ulcers, unusual bruising or yellow skin: call 111 or your doctor straight away
⏳ It takes 2 to 3 months to work fully. Avoid live vaccines

General information, not medical advice. It interacts with allopurinol and febuxostat, so tell your doctor about all your medicines.
