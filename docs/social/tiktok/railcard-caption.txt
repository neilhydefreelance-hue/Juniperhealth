Disabled Persons Railcard: more people can now get one 🚆

💷 A third off most rail fares across Great Britain, for you and one adult with you
🎟️ £20 for 1 year or £54 for 3 years, and you can have it on your phone
✅ You may qualify with PIP or DLA (any rate), Attendance Allowance, a Blue Badge, a disabled bus pass, sight or hearing loss, or epilepsy
🆕 From September 2026, some people with long-term or fluctuating conditions, and neurodivergent people, can apply with professional evidence
📤 Apply on the railcard website and upload proof, such as your award letter
