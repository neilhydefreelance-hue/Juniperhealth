Omeprazole: what to know 💊

🔥 It treats heartburn, acid reflux and stomach ulcers, helps clear H. pylori with antibiotics, and protects your stomach if you take anti-inflammatory painkillers
🧪 It reduces the amount of acid your stomach makes, so ulcers and inflammation can heal
🤕 Common side effects: headache, stomach pain, diarrhoea or constipation, and wind
🩺 See a GP if you need it for more than 2 weeks, have trouble swallowing, are losing weight or are vomiting
📉 Stopping after long use can make heartburn worse for a few weeks. Your GP may suggest lowering the dose slowly
