Schizophrenia: what it is, and what it is not 💜

🧠 It is not a split personality. That is a myth
👂 Symptoms can include hearing voices, strong unusual beliefs, muddled thinking and losing motivation
⏱️ The NHS aims for people with a first episode of psychosis to start treatment within 2 weeks of referral
💊 Treatment can include antipsychotic medicines, CBT for psychosis, family support and physical health checks
🌱 Many people recover well, especially with early treatment and good support
