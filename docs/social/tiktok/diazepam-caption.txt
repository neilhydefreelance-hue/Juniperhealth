Diazepam: short-term only 💊

🩺 Used short term for severe anxiety, muscle spasms, alcohol withdrawal and stopping a long seizure
📅 It can cause dependence even after a few weeks, so it is usually only prescribed for 2 to 4 weeks
🚫 Never take it with alcohol or opioids, as this can stop your breathing
😴 Common side effects: sleepiness, feeling unsteady, poor memory and headache
🛑 Do not stop suddenly after regular use. Your doctor will lower the dose very slowly

Read about diazepam: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. Call 999 for slow or shallow breathing, or if someone is very hard to wake. Do not drive if you feel sleepy.
