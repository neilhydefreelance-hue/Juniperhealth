Asthma: what to do in an attack 💜

🫁 More than 5 million people in the UK get treatment for asthma
😮‍💨 Symptoms: wheezing, breathlessness, a tight chest and coughing at night
1️⃣ Sit up straight and try to keep calm
2️⃣ Use your reliever as your action plan says. Blue reliever: 1 puff every 30 to 60 seconds, up to 10 puffs
🚨 Call 999 if you do not feel better, feel worse, or are too breathless to speak
📋 Ask for a written asthma action plan and a yearly review
