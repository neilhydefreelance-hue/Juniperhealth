Pramipexole: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats Parkinson's disease and moderate to severe restless legs syndrome
😴 Common side effects: feeling sick, sleepiness, dizziness, swollen legs and seeing things that are not there
⚠️ Watch for new urges, such as gambling, shopping, eating or a high sex drive. Tell your doctor straight away. Family may notice first
🚗 It can make you fall asleep without warning. Do not drive if this happens
🛑 Never stop suddenly. Your specialist will lower the dose slowly
