Empagliflozin (Jardiance): what to know 💊

🩺 It is used for type 2 diabetes, heart failure and chronic kidney disease
💧 It makes you pass more sugar, salt and water in your pee, lowering blood sugar and protecting the heart and kidneys
🤕 Common side effects: thrush or genital infections, urine infections, peeing more and thirst
🤒 Sick day rules: stop it if you are unwell and cannot eat or drink, and restart when you are better
🚨 Get urgent help for being sick, stomach pain, fast breathing or sleepiness, or painful swollen genitals with a fever
