Living with kidney disease 🫘

🩺 Protect your kidneys: take your blood pressure medicines, eat less salt, stop smoking and keep active
⚠️ Avoid anti-inflammatory painkillers such as ibuprofen unless your doctor says they are safe
💊 Tell every doctor, dentist and pharmacist you have CKD, and ask about sick day rules
🥗 Mild CKD just needs a healthy, balanced diet. Do not cut potassium or phosphate without advice
💉 Have the flu, COVID-19 and pneumonia vaccines
