Insulin aspart: what to know 💉

⏱️ A fast-acting mealtime insulin. It starts working in about 10 to 20 minutes and lasts 3 to 5 hours
😣 Common side effects: hypos, redness where you inject, lumps under the skin and weight gain
🔄 Change where you inject to avoid lumps, and always carry fast-acting sugar
🚨 A severe hypo with confusion, fits or passing out: someone should give glucagon or call 999
🛑 In type 1 diabetes, never stop insulin, even if you are unwell and not eating. Stopping can cause DKA within hours
