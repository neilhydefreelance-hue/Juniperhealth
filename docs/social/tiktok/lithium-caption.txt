Lithium: what to know 💊

🧠 A mood stabiliser, and one of the most effective treatments for preventing mania and depression in bipolar disorder
😮‍💨 Common side effects: thirst, peeing more, a slight hand shake, feeling sick, a metallic taste and weight gain
🩸 You need regular blood tests for your lithium level, kidneys and thyroid. Carry your lithium record book
💧 Keep fluids and salt steady. Hot weather, sickness and some painkillers such as ibuprofen can raise levels
⚠️ Being sick, diarrhoea, a coarse shake, unsteadiness, slurred speech or confusion can mean too much lithium. Get help straight away

Read about lithium: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. Never stop lithium suddenly. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
