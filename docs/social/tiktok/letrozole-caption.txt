Letrozole (Femara): what to know 💊

🎗️ It is mainly used for breast cancer after the menopause, and also in fertility treatment for PCOS
🧬 It lowers oestrogen. Some breast cancers need oestrogen to grow, so lowering it slows or stops them
🔥 Common side effects: hot flushes, joint pain or stiffness, tiredness and thinning hair
🦴 It can thin your bones. You may have bone scans and be advised to take calcium and vitamin D
🚑 Get help for sudden leg pain or swelling, chest pain or breathlessness. Call 999 for signs of a stroke or heart attack
