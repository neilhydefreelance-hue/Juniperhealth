Adalimumab (Humira): biologic injections explained 💉

🩺 It calms an overactive immune system in rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's and colitis, psoriasis, hidradenitis suppurativa and more
🔬 You will be checked for TB and hepatitis before you start
🦠 Tell your team straight away if you get an infection. Avoid live vaccines and ask about flu, COVID and pneumonia jabs
🔄 Several brands (biosimilars) work in the same way, and your team may switch you between them
🚨 Get help for a high temperature or chills, a cough that will not go away, night sweats or weight loss, or unusual bruising
