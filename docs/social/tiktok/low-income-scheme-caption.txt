Low income? Get help with NHS costs 💷

💰 You can apply to the NHS Low Income Scheme if your savings are below £16,000. Your home does not count
🏠 You can apply even if you work or own your home
✅ An HC2 certificate gives full help: prescriptions, dental treatment, sight tests, glasses and travel to hospital
📄 An HC3 certificate gives some help and shows the most you need to pay
📞 Apply online on the NHS Business Services Authority website, or on form HC1. Call 0300 330 1343 for a form
