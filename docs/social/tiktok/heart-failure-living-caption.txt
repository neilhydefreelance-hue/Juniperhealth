Living with heart failure ❤️

⚖️ Weigh yourself every morning after the toilet and before breakfast. Contact your team if you gain 2kg (about 4 pounds) in 3 days
🧂 Eat less salt, and follow any advice about how much to drink
🚶 Regular, gentle exercise helps. Cardiac rehabilitation will help you build up safely
💉 Have the flu, COVID-19 and pneumonia vaccines
💊 Check with a pharmacist before new medicines. Some, such as ibuprofen, can make heart failure worse
