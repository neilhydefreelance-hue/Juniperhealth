The Work Capability Assessment explained 📋

🏢 It decides whether your health limits your ability to work, for New Style ESA and the Universal Credit health element
📄 The form is ESA50 for ESA or UC50 for Universal Credit. You usually have 4 weeks to send it back
✍️ Describe your bad days, say what happens if you try a task, add letters from your GP or consultant, and keep copies
🔎 Outcomes: fit for work, limited capability for work (LCW), or LCWRA (the support group or health element)
⚖️ Disagree? Ask for a mandatory reconsideration within one month, then appeal to a tribunal
