Eating disorders: you are not alone 💜

💜 Eating disorders are serious mental illnesses, and people can and do recover
📝 Types include anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, ARFID and OSFED
🔍 Warning signs include worrying about food or weight, avoiding eating with others, strict food rules, exercising a lot and mood changes
🩺 See your GP as soon as possible. Early treatment makes recovery more likely
📞 Beat offers free helplines and online support
🚨 Call 999 if someone faints, has chest pain, a very fast or irregular heartbeat, or is very weak or confused
