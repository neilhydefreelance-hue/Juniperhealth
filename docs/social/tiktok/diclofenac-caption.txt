Diclofenac (Voltarol): what to know 💊

🦴 An anti-inflammatory painkiller for arthritis, back pain, sprains, gout attacks and period pain
🧴 The gel eases local pain and swelling. Tablets are not suitable with heart disease, heart failure or after a stroke
📏 Use the lowest dose for the shortest time, not with ibuprofen or naproxen, and it can make asthma worse
🤢 Common side effects: indigestion, stomach pain, feeling sick and headache
🚑 Get help for black or bloody poo or vomiting blood. Call 999 for chest pain or signs of a heart attack or stroke
