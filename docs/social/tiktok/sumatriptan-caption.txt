Sumatriptan: migraine medicine explained 💊

⏱️ It treats migraine and cluster headache attacks, and works best when taken early in an attack
🔥 Common side effects: feeling hot, tingling or heavy, a tight chest or throat, and dizziness
1️⃣ If the first dose did not help, do not take a second dose for the same attack
📅 Taking triptans on 10 or more days a month can cause medication overuse headaches. Your GP can help
🚑 Call 999 if you have severe chest pain or tightness that does not go away

General information, not medical advice. It is not suitable for people with heart disease, peripheral arterial disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure or a past stroke.
