Filling in your PIP form? 5 things to know first 👇

1. It is about how your condition affects you, not your diagnosis
2. Describe your bad days, and how often they happen
3. You can only do something if you can do it safely, well enough, again and again, and in a reasonable time
4. List every aid, even ones you need but do not have yet
5. Send evidence and keep copies of everything
