Can I get PIP? Here are the criteria 👇

1. You are aged 16 to State Pension age
2. Your condition is long term: 3 months so far, and expected to last 9 more
3. You live in England or Wales (Scotland has Adult Disability Payment)
4. You struggle with everyday activities, from preparing food to getting around
5. You score enough points: 8 for the standard rate, 12 for the enhanced rate
6. Your income does not matter. PIP is not means tested, and you can work

Up to £194.60 a week (2026 to 2027 rates).
