High cholesterol has no symptoms 💜

🩸 The only way to know if you have high cholesterol is a blood test
📊 More than half of adults in the UK have cholesterol above the healthy level
❤️ It raises your risk of heart attack and stroke
🧈 Saturated fat, inactivity, smoking, type 2 diabetes and your genes can raise it
🩺 In England, adults aged 40 to 74 get a free NHS Health Check every 5 years
💊 Healthy changes, statins and other medicines can help
