Living with migraine 🧠

⏰ Keep regular routines: the same bedtime, regular meals, plenty of water and steady caffeine
📓 A headache diary can help you spot patterns in attacks, food, sleep and periods
💊 Have a plan: carry your medicine, take it early, and know where you can lie down somewhere dark and quiet
📱 Use night mode, take regular screen breaks, and avoid flickering lights
🏃 Regular, moderate exercise and relaxation can reduce attacks for some people
