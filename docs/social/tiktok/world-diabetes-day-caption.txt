World Diabetes Day, 14 November 2026: act early on diabetes 💙

🚽 Type 1 signs, the 4 Ts: Toilet (peeing more), Thirsty, Tired and Thinner (losing weight without trying)
🚨 4 Ts? See a GP the same day or call NHS 111. Call 999 for being sick, tummy pain, fruity breath, fast breathing or drowsiness
🤫 Type 2 often has no symptoms at first. Signs can include peeing more, thirst, tiredness, thrush or itching and slow-healing cuts
⚠️ Higher risk if you are over 40 (or 25 if South Asian), have a close relative with diabetes, are overweight or have high blood pressure
📊 An HbA1c of 42 to 47 means prediabetes. Healthy changes can stop it becoming type 2
