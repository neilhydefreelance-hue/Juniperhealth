Fluoxetine (Prozac): what to know 💊

🧠 It treats depression, OCD and bulimia, and is the main antidepressant used for young people
⏳ It can take 4 to 6 weeks to work
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, headache, trouble sleeping, feeling restless and less appetite
💊 It stays in your body for weeks, so it interacts with many medicines. Do not take it with St John's wort
🆘 Tell your doctor straight away if you feel worse or have thoughts of suicide, especially if you are under 25
