What is endometriosis? Explained simply 💜

🌸 Tissue similar to the lining of the womb grows in other places, such as the ovaries or bowel, causing pain and scarring
📊 It is thought to affect around 1 in 10 women and people assigned female at birth
😣 Symptoms include severe period pain, pelvic pain, pain during sex, painful poos or pees, heavy periods and extreme tiredness
⏳ Diagnosis takes 8 to 9 years on average. Keep a pain diary, and remember a normal scan does not rule it out
💊 Painkillers, hormone treatments, keyhole surgery and pain management can help
