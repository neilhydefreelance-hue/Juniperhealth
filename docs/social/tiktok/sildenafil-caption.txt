Sildenafil (Viagra): what to know 💊

❤️ Erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease or diabetes. See a GP
🤕 Common side effects: headache, flushing, indigestion, a blocked nose and changes in colour vision
🚫 Never take it with nitrates such as GTN spray or isosorbide, or "poppers". Your blood pressure can drop dangerously
🏥 An erection lasting more than 2 hours: go to A and E
🚑 Chest pain during or after sex: stop and call 999
