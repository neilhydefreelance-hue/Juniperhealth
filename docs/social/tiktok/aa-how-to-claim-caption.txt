How to claim Attendance Allowance, step by step 📋

📞 Claim online at GOV.UK, or call the free helpline on 0800 731 0122. Ask by phone and your claim can start that day, if you send the form back within 6 weeks
✍️ Describe a typical week, include bad days and nights, think about safety, and say how often you need help
💡 It is about the help you need, not only the help you get
📎 Send evidence: GP or consultant letters, your medicines list, a care plan, or a note from someone who helps
⏩ Terminally ill? Use the special rules with an SR1 form and get the higher rate straight away
