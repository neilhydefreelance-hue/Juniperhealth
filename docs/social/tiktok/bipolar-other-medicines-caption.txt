Bipolar medicines, part 2 of 2: antipsychotics and other medicines 💊💜

⚡ For mania: olanzapine, quetiapine, risperidone, haloperidol or aripiprazole
🌧️ For bipolar depression: quetiapine, olanzapine with fluoxetine, lamotrigine, and sometimes lurasidone
😌 A benzodiazepine, such as lorazepam or diazepam, is sometimes used for a few days for agitation or sleep
⚠️ Antidepressants on their own can trigger mania or hypomania, so they are usually only used alongside a mood stabiliser or antipsychotic
⚖️ Watch for weight gain, sleepiness, blood sugar and cholesterol changes, and have a physical health check every year
