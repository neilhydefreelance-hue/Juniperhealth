Living with Parkinson's 💜

🚶 Regular exercise is one of the best things you can do: walking, cycling, dance, tai chi or boxing for Parkinson's
🏠 Turn slowly, keep your home clutter free, and ask about a falls assessment
🥦 Eat fibre and drink fluids. Trouble swallowing? Ask for a speech and language therapy referral
😴 Depression, anxiety and sleep problems are part of Parkinson's, not a sign of weakness. They can be treated
🚗 You must tell the DVLA. Many people carry on driving for years

General information, not medical advice. Parkinson's UK runs a free helpline, local groups and exercise classes.
