Rosuvastatin: what to know 💊

❤️ A strong statin that lowers cholesterol and reduces your risk of a heart attack or stroke
😣 Common side effects: headache, muscle aches, stomach pain, feeling sick, constipation and feeling weak
⚠️ Call 111 or your doctor if you get unexplained muscle pain or weakness, especially with a high temperature or dark pee
⏰ Take indigestion remedies at least 2 hours apart from it
📈 No withdrawal symptoms, but if you stop, your cholesterol rises again
