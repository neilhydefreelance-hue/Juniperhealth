Living with eczema 🧴

🚿 Have short, warm baths or showers. Wash with your emollient and apply more straight after
👕 Wear soft cotton and use non-biological washing powder
✋ Keep nails short, and try tapping or pressing the skin instead of scratching
🔥 Emollients soak into fabrics and make them catch fire more easily, including paraffin-free ones. Keep away from flames and do not smoke
💭 Talk to your GP if you are struggling. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies

General information, not medical advice. The National Eczema Society has a helpline.
