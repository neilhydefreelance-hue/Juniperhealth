Locked accessible toilets? How to get in 🚻

🔑 A Radar key opens over 10,000 locked accessible toilets across the UK
💷 Buy a genuine one from Disability Rights UK for about £6. Some councils give them out free
⚠️ Cheap copies online may not work
♿ Changing Places toilets have a hoist, a height-adjustable changing bench and space for up to two carers
🌻 Handy cards: Just Can't Wait card, Sunflower lanyard and the "Please offer me a seat" badge
