Osteoarthritis or rheumatoid arthritis? 💜

🦴 Osteoarthritis: the smooth layer in the joint wears thinner, often in the knees, hips and hands
🛡️ Rheumatoid arthritis: the immune system attacks the joint lining, often in the hands, wrists and feet on both sides
🌅 Morning stiffness usually eases within 30 minutes in osteoarthritis, but lasts longer in rheumatoid arthritis
💪 Exercise, managing weight and anti-inflammatory gels help osteoarthritis
⏱️ Rheumatoid arthritis needs urgent referral. Medicines like methotrexate can help
