Clopidogrel: what to know 💊

🩸 It stops platelets sticking together, lowering the risk of heart attacks and strokes, including after a stroke, heart attack or stent
🤕 Common side effects: bruising easily, nosebleeds, bleeding for longer after a cut and indigestion
⚠️ Never stop early, especially within a year of a stent. Talk to your heart doctor first, including before surgery or dental work
💊 Some indigestion medicines, such as omeprazole and esomeprazole, can make it work less well. Ask a pharmacist
🚑 Call 999 for a severe headache or sudden weakness
