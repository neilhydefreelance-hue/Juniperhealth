Free NHS eye tests and help with glasses 👓

✅ Free sight test if you are under 16 or 60+, have diabetes or glaucoma, are sight impaired, get certain benefits or have an HC2 certificate
👨‍👩‍👧 Also free if you are 40 or over and your parent, brother, sister or child has glaucoma
🏠 Cannot leave home without help? Ask a local optician for a free NHS home visit
🎟️ An NHS optical voucher pays towards glasses or contact lenses for some people
⚠️ Being over 60 on its own does not give you a voucher
