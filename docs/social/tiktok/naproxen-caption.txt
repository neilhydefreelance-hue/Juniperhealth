Naproxen: what to know 💊

🦴 An anti-inflammatory painkiller for arthritis, gout attacks, back pain, sprains and period pain
📏 Take the lowest dose for the shortest time. Your doctor may also give you a stomach-protecting medicine
🤢 Common side effects: indigestion, stomach pain, feeling sick and headache
🚫 Not with other anti-inflammatories or blood thinners unless your doctor advises it, and it can make asthma worse
🚑 Get help for black or bloody poo or vomiting blood. Call 999 for chest pain or signs of a heart attack or stroke
