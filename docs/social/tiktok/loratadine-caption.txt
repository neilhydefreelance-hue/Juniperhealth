Loratadine: what to know 💊

🤧 A non-drowsy antihistamine for hay fever, other allergies and itchy hives
🧪 It blocks histamine, easing sneezing, itching, a runny nose and watery eyes
😴 Common side effects: headache, tiredness and some sleepiness (less than older antihistamines)
⚠️ Ask a pharmacist first if you have liver problems or epilepsy
🚨 Call 111 or your doctor if you get a fast or irregular heartbeat, or fits
