Gabapentin: what to know 💊

🩺 It treats nerve pain, such as after shingles or from diabetes, and some types of epilepsy
😴 Common side effects: feeling sleepy or tired, dizzy or unsteady, weight gain and swollen hands or feet
⚠️ It is a controlled drug and can cause dependence. Do not drive if it makes you sleepy or dizzy
🚑 Taking it with opioids, alcohol or other sedatives raises the risk of dangerous breathing problems. Call 999 for slow or shallow breathing
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly
