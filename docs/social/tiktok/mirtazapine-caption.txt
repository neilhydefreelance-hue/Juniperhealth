Mirtazapine: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats depression, and can also help with sleep and appetite
🌙 It makes you sleepy, so it is usually taken at bedtime. Lower doses can be more sedating. Avoid alcohol
🍽️ Common side effects: sleepiness at first, more appetite, weight gain, dry mouth and headache
🩺 Contact your doctor if you get a high temperature, sore throat, mouth ulcers or yellow skin or eyes
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly
