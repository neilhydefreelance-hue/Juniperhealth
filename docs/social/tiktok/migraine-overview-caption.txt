Migraine is more than a headache 💜

📊 Around 1 in 5 women and 1 in 15 men have migraine. Attacks usually last 4 hours to 3 days
🤕 Symptoms include a throbbing headache, feeling sick and sensitivity to light and sound
⚡ About 1 in 3 get an aura first, like zigzag lines or pins and needles
📓 A headache diary helps spot triggers like hormones, stress, tiredness and skipped meals
💊 Painkillers, triptans, preventive medicines and CGRP medicines can help
🚨 Call 999 for a sudden, worst ever headache, or one with weakness, slurred speech, confusion or a drooping face
