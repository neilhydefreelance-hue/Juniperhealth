ESA explained 💜

💼 ESA is about work: does your health limit how much you can work?
🧾 New Style ESA is based on the National Insurance you have paid. Savings and your partner's income do not matter
💷 Up to £145.90 a week in the support group, or up to £95.55 in the first 13 weeks and the work-related activity group (2026 to 2027)
⏳ The support group has no time limit. The work-related activity group is usually up to 365 days
🧠 No diagnosis needed. Physical and mental health count equally
➕ You can get ESA and PIP at the same time
