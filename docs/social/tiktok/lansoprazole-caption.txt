Lansoprazole: what to know 💊

🔥 It reduces stomach acid, for heartburn, acid reflux and stomach ulcers, and to protect the stomach
😣 Common side effects: headache, diarrhoea or constipation, feeling sick, stomach pain and wind
🧪 Long-term use can lower vitamin B12 and magnesium levels
🩺 See a GP if you have difficulty swallowing, weight loss or are being sick
📉 After long use, heartburn can be worse for a few weeks when you stop. Your GP may suggest stepping down slowly
