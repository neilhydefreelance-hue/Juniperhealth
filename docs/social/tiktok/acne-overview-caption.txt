Acne: you do not have to put up with it 💜

🧼 Acne is not caused by being dirty. Washing too often or scrubbing can make it worse
👩 It is most common between 12 and 25, but many adults, especially women, have acne too
💊 Benzoyl peroxide from a pharmacy, prescription gels, antibiotic tablets with a gel, or isotretinoin from a specialist can help
⏳ Treatments usually take 6 to 8 weeks or more, so stick with them
🩺 See a GP early if acne is scarring, not improving or affecting how you feel
