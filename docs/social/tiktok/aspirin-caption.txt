Low-dose aspirin: who needs it? 💊

🩸 Usually 75mg, it stops platelets sticking together so clots are less likely. It helps after a heart attack, stroke or mini-stroke
🚫 It is no longer recommended for people who do not have heart or circulation disease
👶 Never give aspirin to under 16s, except on a doctor's advice, because of the risk of Reye's syndrome
🤕 Side effects: indigestion, bruising easily, bleeding for longer, and it can make asthma worse
⚠️ If you take it for heart disease or after a stroke, do not stop without advice
