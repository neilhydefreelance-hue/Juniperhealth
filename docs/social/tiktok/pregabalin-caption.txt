Pregabalin (Lyrica): what to know 💊

🩺 It treats nerve pain, generalised anxiety disorder and some types of epilepsy
😴 Common side effects: feeling sleepy or dizzy, weight gain, blurred vision, dry mouth and swollen hands or feet
⚠️ It is a controlled drug and can cause dependence. Tell your doctor if you need more to get the same effect
🚑 Taking it with opioids, alcohol or other sedatives can stop your breathing. Call 999 for slow or shallow breathing
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly
