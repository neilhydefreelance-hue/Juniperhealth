Quetiapine: what to know 💊

🧠 An antipsychotic for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and depression alongside an antidepressant
😴 Common side effects: sleepiness, dizziness, dry mouth, weight gain and constipation
🩺 Your weight, blood sugar and cholesterol will be checked regularly
🚨 Call 999 for a high temperature with stiff muscles and confusion
🛑 Never stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose slowly

Read about quetiapine: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. Do not drive if you feel sleepy. If you have thoughts of harming yourself, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
