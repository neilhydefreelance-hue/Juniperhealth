Living with epilepsy ⚡

🧩 Common triggers: missing medicine, lack of sleep, stress, alcohol, illness, and flashing lights for a small number of people
🚿 Have showers rather than baths, and swim with someone who knows about your epilepsy
🚗 You must tell the DVLA and stop driving. You can usually reapply for a car licence after 12 months without a seizure
🤰 Planning a baby? Talk to your specialist first. Do not stop your medicine suddenly
🚨 Call 999 if a seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, or one seizure follows another
