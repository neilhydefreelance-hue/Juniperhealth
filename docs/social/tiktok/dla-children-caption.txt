DLA for children explained 💜

🧒 For children under 16 who need much more care, supervision or help getting around than other children their age
🤲 The care part has 3 rates and can be paid from 3 months old
🚶 The mobility part: higher rate from age 3, lower rate from age 5
💷 Your income and savings do not matter
➕ It can lead to Carer's Allowance, more Universal Credit, a Blue Badge, Motability and Family Fund grants
