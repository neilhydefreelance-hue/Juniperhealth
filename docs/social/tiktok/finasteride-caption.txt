Finasteride: what to know 💊

🚹 It shrinks an enlarged prostate (5mg). A lower dose (1mg) treats male pattern hair loss
🤕 Common side effects: erection problems, lower sex drive, less semen and breast tenderness
⚠️ The medicines regulator warns about depression and suicidal thoughts. Stop and see a doctor if your mood changes
🤰 Women who are or may become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets
🔬 It lowers PSA test results
