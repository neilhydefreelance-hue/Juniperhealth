Fatty liver: common, silent and reversible 💜

📊 Fatty liver disease (MASLD, once called NAFLD) is thought to affect up to 1 in 5 people in the UK
🤫 It usually has no symptoms and is often found by chance on a blood test or scan
⚠️ Higher risk with extra weight around the waist, type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, being over 50 or smoking
🔬 Tests include blood tests, a FIB-4 score, an ELF blood test and a FibroScan
🔄 In the early stages, losing weight, being active and eating healthily can reduce fat and even reverse some scarring
