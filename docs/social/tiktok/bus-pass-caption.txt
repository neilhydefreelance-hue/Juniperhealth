Free bus travel if you are disabled 🚌

🏴 England: free local buses from 9:30am to 11pm on weekdays, and all day at weekends and bank holidays
🏴 Wales: free bus travel at any time with a Concessionary Travel Card
✅ You may qualify if you are blind or partially sighted, severely deaf, find walking very hard, have a learning disability or cannot speak
📄 8 PIP points in moving around or communicating verbally, or higher rate DLA mobility, is usually enough proof
👫 Some councils, and Wales, offer a companion pass so someone can travel with you for free

Read about bus passes: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Apply to your council in England, or Transport for Wales.
