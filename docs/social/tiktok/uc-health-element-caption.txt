The Universal Credit health element explained 💜

💷 Extra Universal Credit if your health means you cannot work or prepare for work (LCWRA)
⬆️ Higher rate: £429.80 a month, for people who had it before 6 April 2026, people who are terminally ill, and people who meet the severe conditions criteria
⬇️ Lower rate: £217.26 a month, for most people who start getting it on or after 6 April 2026
🛡️ Already getting it? You are protected and keep the higher rate while you still qualify
⏳ There is usually a 3 month waiting period. PIP does not reduce your Universal Credit
