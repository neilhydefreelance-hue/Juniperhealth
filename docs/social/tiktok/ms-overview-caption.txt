What is MS? Explained simply 💜

🧠 In multiple sclerosis, the immune system attacks the protective layer around nerves in the brain and spinal cord
📊 More than 150,000 people in the UK have MS. It is often diagnosed in people in their 20s, 30s and 40s
😴 Symptoms include fatigue, sight problems, numbness, weakness, balance and bladder problems
🔁 Types include relapsing remitting, secondary progressive and primary progressive MS
💊 Disease modifying therapies, relapse treatment, fatigue courses and physiotherapy can help
