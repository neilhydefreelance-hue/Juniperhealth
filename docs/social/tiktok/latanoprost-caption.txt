Latanoprost: glaucoma eye drops 👁️

💧 It lowers pressure inside the eye and is often the first treatment for glaucoma
🌙 Use it once a day in the evening, remove contact lenses first, and keep unopened bottles in the fridge
👀 Common side effects: red or irritated eyes, longer and darker eyelashes, the iris slowly darkening, and darker skin around the eye
⚠️ Keep using it. Eye pressure rises again within days if you stop, and untreated glaucoma can permanently damage your sight
🩺 Get help for eye pain, swelling or sudden changes in vision
