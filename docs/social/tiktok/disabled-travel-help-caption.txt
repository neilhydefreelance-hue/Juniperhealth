Railcards and free bus passes for disabled people 💜

🚆 A Disabled Persons Railcard gives a third off most train fares, for you and one adult with you. It costs £20 a year
✅ You can get one with PIP or DLA (any rate), Attendance Allowance, a Blue Badge, epilepsy, or if you are deaf or sight impaired
🆕 Since September 2026, some people with long-term or fluctuating conditions and some neurodivergent people can apply too
🚌 England: a disabled person's bus pass gives free local bus travel from 9:30am on weekdays and all day at weekends
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Wales: a Concessionary Travel Card gives free bus travel at any time
