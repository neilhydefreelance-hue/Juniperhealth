Dementia: early signs to look for 💜

🧠 Around 980,000 people in the UK live with dementia
📝 Early signs include forgetting recent events, struggling to find words, confusion about time or place, trouble with money and mood changes
🔍 Types include Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, dementia with Lewy bodies and frontotemporal dementia
⏳ Dementia that starts before 65 is called young onset dementia
🩺 Worried? See a GP. They can do memory and blood tests and refer you to a memory clinic
🤝 Medicines, cognitive stimulation therapy and Admiral Nurses for families can help
