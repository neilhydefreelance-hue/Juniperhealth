What is fibromyalgia? Explained simply 💜

🌿 Long-term pain all over the body, plus extreme tiredness, poor sleep and fibro fog
📊 The NHS says it may affect as many as 1 in 20 people in the UK
🧠 Experts think the brain and nerves process pain signals differently
🩺 There is no single test, so a GP looks at your symptoms and rules out other conditions
💪 Gentle exercise, pacing, talking therapies and some medicines can help
📝 You may be able to get PIP, based on how it affects your daily life
