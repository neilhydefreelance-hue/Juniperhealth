How to claim DLA for your child, step by step 📋

📞 Download the DLA1 Child form from GOV.UK, or call the free helpline on 0800 121 4600. Send it back within 6 weeks and your claim starts from the day you asked
👧 DLA is about the extra help your child needs compared with other children the same age
📓 Keep a diary for a week or two, describe bad days and nights, explain the risks, and say how long things take
📎 Send evidence: letters from their GP, consultant or therapist, an EHCP or school support plan, and a letter from their teacher or SENCo
⏸️ The form is long. Take breaks, do it over a few days, and keep a copy
