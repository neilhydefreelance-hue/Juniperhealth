Living with IBS 🫄

📓 Keep a diary of what you eat, how you feel and your symptoms for a few weeks
🍽️ Eat slowly and regularly, drink plenty of water, and do not cut out whole food groups without advice
🧘 The gut and brain are closely linked. Relaxation, exercise, mindfulness and good sleep can help
🔑 A Radar key opens thousands of locked accessible toilets
🚻 A "Can't Wait" card can help you ask to use a toilet in shops and cafes

Read about IBS: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. The IBS Network has a helpline and self-care plan.
