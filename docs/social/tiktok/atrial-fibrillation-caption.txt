What is atrial fibrillation (AF)? 💜

❤️ AF causes an irregular and often fast heartbeat
📊 Around 1.5 million people in the UK have AF, and many more may not know
💓 Symptoms include a fluttering or racing heart, breathlessness, tiredness and dizziness. Some people have none
🧠 Clots can form and travel to the brain, causing a stroke. Treatment lowers this risk
📈 An ECG confirms AF, even if your smart watch spotted it first
🚨 Call 999 for chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or signs of a stroke
