MCAS: what is mast cell activation syndrome? 🛡️

🧬 Mast cells, part of your immune system, release chemicals such as histamine too easily
🔥 Symptoms come and go: flushing, itching or hives, tummy pain, a fast heartbeat, dizziness and brain fog
🌡️ Common triggers include heat, alcohol, some foods, stress, exercise and strong smells
📓 There is no single test. Keep a symptom diary and photos of rashes or flushing to show your GP
🚨 Call 999 for swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, or difficulty breathing. Use your adrenaline auto-injector first
