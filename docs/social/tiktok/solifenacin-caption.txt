Solifenacin (Vesicare): overactive bladder 💊

🚻 It reduces sudden urges to pee, needing to pee often and leaking
👄 Common side effects: dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision and dry eyes
🧓 Long-term use of this type of medicine has been linked with memory problems in older people. Ask if another option suits you better
🩺 Get help if you cannot pee, have severe stomach pain or swelling, or have confusion or memory problems
