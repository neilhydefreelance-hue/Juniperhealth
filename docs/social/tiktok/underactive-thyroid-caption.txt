Underactive thyroid explained 💜

🦋 Your thyroid makes hormones that control how fast your body uses energy
😴 Signs can include tiredness, weight gain, feeling cold, constipation, thinning hair, low mood and poor memory
👩 It is most common in women over 60. Hashimoto's is the most common cause in the UK
🩸 A simple TSH and T4 blood test shows how your thyroid is working
💊 Most people take a daily tablet called levothyroxine
🤰 Pregnant or planning a pregnancy? Tell your GP straight away
