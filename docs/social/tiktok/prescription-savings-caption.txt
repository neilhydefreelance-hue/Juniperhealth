Paying for prescriptions? You might not need to 💊

📊 Around 9 in 10 prescription items in England are given free
📋 A medical exemption certificate covers diabetes treated with medicine, an underactive thyroid, epilepsy, cancer treatment and some disabilities
✅ It makes all your NHS prescriptions free. Ask your GP surgery for form FP92A
💷 A 12 month prepayment certificate saves money if you need 12 or more items a year, and you can pay in 10 monthly instalments
