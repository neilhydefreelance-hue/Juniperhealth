Allopurinol: stopping gout coming back 💊

🦶 Taken every day, it lowers uric acid, stops gout attacks and prevents some kidney stones. It is usually taken for life
⚠️ It does not treat an attack that has started. Once you are on it, keep taking it during a flare
📈 You may get more gout flares in the first few months. Drink plenty of fluids
🔴 Any skin rash, especially with a high temperature, sore mouth or blistering, can be serious. Stop and get help straight away
💊 It interacts with azathioprine and mercaptopurine. Your doctor must adjust these
