Tolterodine: what to know 💊

🚻 It treats an overactive bladder, reducing urgency, needing to pee often and leaking
😣 Common side effects: dry mouth, constipation, headache, dry eyes, blurred vision and dizziness
⚠️ Call 111 or your doctor if you cannot pee, have a fast or irregular heartbeat, or feel confused
🧠 Long-term use of this type of medicine has been linked with memory problems in older people
🔁 No withdrawal symptoms, but bladder symptoms may come back when you stop
