Insulin glargine: background insulin explained 💉

🕐 Usually injected once a day, it releases insulin evenly for about 24 hours to keep blood sugar steady
📉 Common side effects: hypos, redness where you inject, lumps under the skin and weight gain
⚠️ Toujeo is stronger and not dose for dose the same as Lantus. Do not mix glargine with other insulins
🛑 Never stop without medical advice. It can quickly cause very high blood sugar and, in type 1 diabetes, diabetic ketoacidosis
🚑 Get urgent help for a severe hypo with confusion, fits or passing out
