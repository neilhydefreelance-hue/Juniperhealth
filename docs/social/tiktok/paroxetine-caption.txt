Paroxetine: what to know 💊

🧠 An SSRI antidepressant for depression, panic and social anxiety, generalised anxiety, OCD and PTSD
😣 Common side effects: feeling sick, sweating, sleep changes, dry mouth, sexual problems and weight gain
⚠️ It can cause stronger withdrawal than many antidepressants. Your doctor will lower the dose very slowly
🤰 Not usually recommended in pregnancy. Talk to your doctor before trying for a baby, but do not stop suddenly
🆘 Tell your doctor straight away if you feel worse or have thoughts of suicide, especially if you are under 25
