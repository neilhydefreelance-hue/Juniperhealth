Spironolactone: what to know 💊

❤️ It is used for heart failure, fluid build-up and blood pressure that is hard to control
✨ It is also used for acne in women and excess hair growth, including in PCOS
🍌 Watch your potassium: you need regular blood tests, and should avoid potassium supplements and salt substitutes
🤕 Common side effects: breast tenderness, irregular periods, dizziness and peeing more
🩺 Get help for muscle weakness or numbness, an irregular heartbeat, confusion or peeing much less
