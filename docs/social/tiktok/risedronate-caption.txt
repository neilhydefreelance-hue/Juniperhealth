Risedronate: stronger bones 💊

🦴 It slows the cells that break down bone, so bones become denser and the risk of fractures falls
🌅 Take it first thing in the morning with a full glass of plain water, 30 minutes before food or drink, and stay upright for 30 minutes
🦷 Have regular dental check-ups and tell your dentist you take it. Get enough calcium and vitamin D
🩺 Get help for pain swallowing or new heartburn, jaw pain or a loose tooth, or new thigh, hip or groin pain
⏸️ Your doctor may suggest a break after about 5 years, depending on your fracture risk
