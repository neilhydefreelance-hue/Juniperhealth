Methotrexate: once a week only 💊

🦴 It treats rheumatoid arthritis, severe psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis and Crohn's disease
📅 Take it ONCE A WEEK, on the same day each week. Taking it daily by mistake can be fatal
💊 You usually take folic acid on a different day, need regular blood tests and should limit alcohol
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, mouth ulcers, tiredness, hair thinning and more infections
🩺 Contact your doctor for a new dry cough or breathlessness, a high temperature or sore throat, unusual bruising, or yellow skin or eyes
