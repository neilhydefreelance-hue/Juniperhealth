Escitalopram (Cipralex): what to know 💊

🧠 It treats depression, generalised anxiety, panic and social anxiety, OCD and PTSD
⏳ It usually takes 4 to 6 weeks to work fully. Some people feel more anxious at first
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, headache, sweating, sleep changes and yawning
💓 It can affect heart rhythm. No St John's wort, and do not stop suddenly
🆘 Tell your doctor straight away if you feel worse or have thoughts of suicide
