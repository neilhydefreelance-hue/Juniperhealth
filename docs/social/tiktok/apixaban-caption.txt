Apixaban: blood thinner basics 💊

🩸 It prevents strokes in atrial fibrillation, treats and prevents DVT and PE, and prevents clots after hip or knee surgery
🤕 Common side effects: bruising easily, nosebleeds, bleeding gums and heavier periods
🪪 Carry an anticoagulant alert card and tell any doctor, dentist or pharmacist you take it
🚫 No ibuprofen or aspirin unless your doctor says so
⚠️ Never stop it suddenly, including before surgery or dental work, without talking to your doctor
