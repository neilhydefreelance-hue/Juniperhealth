Paracetamol: how to use it safely 💊

🔢 No more than 4g in 24 hours, usually 8 x 500mg tablets, with at least 4 hours between doses
🏷️ Check the label: cold and flu remedies and co-codamol can also contain paracetamol
⚖️ People who weigh less than 50kg may need a lower dose. Ask a pharmacist
🤕 Taking painkillers for headaches on more than 15 days a month can cause medication overuse headaches
🏥 Taken too much? Go to A and E straight away, even if you feel well
