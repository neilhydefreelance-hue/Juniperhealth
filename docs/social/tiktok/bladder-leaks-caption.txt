Bladder leaks: very common, very treatable 💜

💧 Urinary incontinence affects millions of women and men in the UK
😊 Many people feel embarrassed, but it is usually treatable
📝 Types include stress, urge, mixed and overflow incontinence
🤰 Causes include pregnancy, childbirth, the menopause, being overweight and prostate problems
💪 Pelvic floor exercises, bladder training and medicines can help
🩺 See a GP, or refer yourself to a continence service in some areas
