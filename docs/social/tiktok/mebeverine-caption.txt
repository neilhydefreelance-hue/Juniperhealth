Mebeverine (Colofac): for IBS cramps 💊

🌀 It relaxes the muscles in the bowel, easing stomach cramps and bloating in IBS
👍 Side effects are rare. Some people get indigestion or heartburn
⚠️ Get help for a skin rash or swelling of the face, which can be an allergic reaction
🩺 See a GP if you have new bowel symptoms over 40, blood in your poo, weight loss, or a change that lasts more than a few weeks
