Furosemide: water tablets explained 💊

💧 It removes extra fluid from the body in heart failure and other conditions that cause swelling
🌅 Take it in the morning so you are not up at night to pee
⚖️ Heart failure? Weigh yourself daily and tell your team about sudden weight gain
😵 Common side effects: peeing more, feeling dizzy or light-headed, thirst and muscle cramps
🩺 Get help for signs of dehydration, an irregular heartbeat or muscle weakness, or ringing in the ears
