Disabled? Help to get through winter 2026 to 2027 💜

🔥 Heat the rooms you use most to at least 18°C if you can, wear thin layers and have hot drinks
💷 Warm Home Discount: £150 off your electricity bill, usually automatic with a means-tested benefit
❄️ Cold Weather Payments: £25 for each 7 day spell of very cold weather, from 1 November to 31 March
💉 Free flu jabs for many people with long-term conditions, people 65 or over, and carers
📋 Join the Priority Services Register, order prescriptions early, and call 105 in a power cut

General information, not financial or medical advice. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
