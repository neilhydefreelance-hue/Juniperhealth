Bisoprolol: what to know 💊

❤️ A beta blocker for heart failure, angina, high blood pressure and fast heart rhythms
🫀 It blocks adrenaline on the heart, slowing your heart rate so it works more efficiently
😴 Common side effects: feeling tired or dizzy, cold hands and feet, headache and feeling sick
🫁 It can make asthma worse and can hide signs of low blood sugar if you have diabetes
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Your doctor will lower the dose over 1 to 2 weeks

General information, not medical advice. Get help for chest pain, fainting or a very slow heartbeat.
