What is psoriasis? Explained simply 💜

🔬 Skin cells are made too fast, causing patches of flaky skin with silvery scales
📊 Around 1 in 50 people in the UK have psoriasis
📍 It often appears on the elbows, knees, scalp, lower back and nails
⚡ Triggers include stress, skin injuries, throat infections, smoking and alcohol
🦴 Up to 1 in 3 get psoriatic arthritis, so tell your GP about joint pain
🧴 Emollients, vitamin D and steroid creams, light therapy, tablets and biologics can help
