Asthma: work and benefits 🫁

⚖️ Asthma can count as a disability. The law looks at how you would be without your medicine, so many people are covered
🏭 Ask for adjustments, like working away from dust and fumes, a smoke-free workplace, good ventilation and time for appointments
👷 Asthma caused by work? Tell your employer and GP. You may be able to claim Industrial Injuries Disablement Benefit
📝 Severe asthma can affect PIP activities like moving around, washing, dressing and preparing food
💊 Prescriptions are not free for asthma in England, but a prepayment certificate can save money. Free in Wales
