Methylphenidate: ADHD medicine explained 💊

🧠 It is the most common ADHD medicine and helps with concentration, impulsiveness and hyperactivity, from age 6 to adults
🍽️ Common side effects: less appetite, trouble sleeping, headache, stomach ache and a fast heartbeat
📏 Height, weight, blood pressure and heart rate are checked regularly
🔄 Long-acting brands release the medicine differently. Do not switch without advice
🚨 Get help for chest pain or fainting, seeing or hearing things, new tics, or thoughts of self-harm
