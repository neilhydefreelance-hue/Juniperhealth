Mometasone: what to know 💊

🌼 A steroid used as a nasal spray for hay fever and nasal polyps, a cream for eczema and psoriasis, and an asthma inhaler
😣 Common side effects: nosebleeds, a dry or sore nose, headache, and stinging on the skin
🧴 Strong steroid creams can thin the skin with long use
⚠️ Call 111 or your doctor if you notice changes in your eyesight, or weeping or crusting skin
📉 Skin may flare if a strong cream is stopped suddenly after long use. Your doctor may suggest using it less often first
