Ezetimibe: another way to lower cholesterol 💊

🍳 It reduces how much cholesterol your gut absorbs from food, so LDL ("bad") cholesterol falls
💊 It is often used with a statin, or instead of one, and also treats inherited high cholesterol
🤕 Common side effects: stomach pain, diarrhoea, wind, tiredness and muscle aches
🥗 It works best with a healthy diet. Cholesterol rises again if you stop
🩺 Get help for unexplained muscle pain or weakness, yellow skin or eyes, or severe stomach pain
