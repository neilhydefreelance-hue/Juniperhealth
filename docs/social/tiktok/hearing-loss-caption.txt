Hearing loss: know the signs 💜

👂 Around 1 in 3 adults in the UK are deaf, have hearing loss or have tinnitus (RNID)
🗣️ Signs include finding conversations hard, asking people to repeat, turning the TV up and missing the doorbell
🩺 See your GP for a referral to NHS audiology. In some areas you can refer yourself
🦻 Hearing aids are free on the NHS
🔔 Flashing doorbells, captioned phones, hearing loops and lipreading classes can help too
⚠️ Sudden hearing loss? Call NHS 111 or see a GP the same day
