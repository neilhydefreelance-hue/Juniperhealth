Macular degeneration (AMD) explained 💜

👁️ AMD affects your central, detailed sight, like reading and seeing faces
📊 It is the most common cause of sight loss in the UK
🐢 Dry AMD develops slowly. Wet AMD can damage sight quickly, but can be treated
👓 Signs include blurred central sight, straight lines looking wavy and a dark patch
💜 Seeing things that are not there (Charles Bonnet syndrome) is common and not a mental health problem
🚭 Smoking more than doubles the risk
🚨 Sudden change in your central sight? See an optician or eye casualty the same day
