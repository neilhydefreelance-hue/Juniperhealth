Anxious and low at the same time? 💜

🧠 Mixed anxiety and depression is one of the most common mental health problems in the UK
😔 Signs can include feeling low or hopeless, worried or on edge, tired, poor sleep and finding it hard to concentrate
💷 Money worries, bereavement, long-term pain or illness and loneliness can all play a part
🗣️ Talk to your GP. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies. In Wales, ask your GP about local support
🆘 If you feel unsafe, call 999 in an emergency, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time
