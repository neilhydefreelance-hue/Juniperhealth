What is Parkinson's? More than a tremor 💜

🧠 Nerve cells that make dopamine, which helps control movement, are slowly lost
📊 Around 153,000 people in the UK have Parkinson's
✋ Main symptoms: tremor (often in one hand), slow movement, smaller handwriting and stiff muscles
🔍 It also affects balance, voice, sense of smell, sleep, mood and bowels
⏰ Taking medicines at exactly the right times really matters, including in hospital
💊 Levodopa, other medicines, physiotherapy and speech therapy can help
