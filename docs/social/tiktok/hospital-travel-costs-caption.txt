Claim back hospital travel costs 🚌

✅ You can claim if you get income-related ESA, Pension Credit Guarantee Credit, Universal Credit within the earnings limits, or have an HC2 certificate
🚗 It covers the cheapest reasonable way there, such as a bus fare, or mileage and parking. Sometimes a companion too
🧾 Ask at the hospital cashier or patient services. Keep tickets and receipts. You usually have up to 3 months
🅿️ Many hospitals offer free parking for Blue Badge holders and people who visit often
