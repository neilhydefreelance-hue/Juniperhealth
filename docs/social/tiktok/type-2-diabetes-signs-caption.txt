Type 2 diabetes: know the signs 💜

📊 About 9 in 10 people with diabetes in the UK have type 2
⚠️ Many people have no symptoms at first
🚻 Possible signs: peeing more, thirst, tiredness, weight loss, repeated thrush, slow healing cuts and blurred vision
👪 You are more at risk if you are over 40 (over 25 if South Asian), have a family history, are overweight or have high blood pressure
🩸 A simple HbA1c blood test shows your average blood sugar
