The Timms Review of PIP explained 💜

🔍 It is a review of how PIP works
🤝 It is led by 3 co-chairs, including Sir Stephen Timms, and almost everyone on its steering group has lived experience of disability or long-term ill health
📬 38,713 people responded to the call for evidence
📝 People raised assessments, fluctuating conditions, stress, paperwork and delays
💡 Emerging ideas include fewer reviews for lifelong conditions, clearer letters, better use of evidence, reasonable adjustments and advocacy
⏳ The final report is due in autumn 2026. Nothing changes yet, so you can still claim PIP in the usual way
