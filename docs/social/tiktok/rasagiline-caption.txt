Rasagiline: what to know 💊

🧠 A once-a-day tablet for Parkinson's. It stops dopamine being broken down in the brain, which improves movement
😣 Common side effects: headache, flu-like symptoms, joint pain, indigestion and low mood
⚠️ Tell your doctor about urges you cannot control, such as gambling or shopping, or seeing things that are not there
💊 It interacts with some antidepressants, tramadol, and cough and cold remedies. Always check with a pharmacist
🔍 Have regular skin checks, and report a new or changing mole
