Memantine: dementia medicine explained 💊

🧠 It treats moderate to severe Alzheimer's disease and can help with memory, attention and daily activities
💊 It can be taken on its own or with donepezil, galantamine or rivastigmine
🤕 Common side effects: headache, feeling dizzy or sleepy, constipation and high blood pressure
🩺 Get help for fits, seeing or hearing things that are not there, sudden confusion or severe stomach pain
📋 Symptoms may get worse after stopping. Decide with the memory clinic or GP
