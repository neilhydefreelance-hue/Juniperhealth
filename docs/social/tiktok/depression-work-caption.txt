Depression and work: your rights 💜

⚖️ Depression can count as a disability if it has a substantial, long-term effect on daily life. The law looks at you without treatment
🕘 Reasonable adjustments can include flexible or later hours, time off for therapy, a phased return and working from home
🤝 Access to Work offers free, confidential Mental Health Support, with up to 9 months of advice and a support plan
📋 PIP can help if you need prompting to eat or wash, reminders for medicines, help with money, or find mixing with people very distressing
💷 Cannot work? You may get New Style ESA or extra Universal Credit, as well as PIP
