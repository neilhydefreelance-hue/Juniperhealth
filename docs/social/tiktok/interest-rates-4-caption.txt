Interest rates are up to 4%: what it means if money is tight 💷

📈 The Bank of England raised Bank Rate from 3.75% to 4% on 5 November 2026, the first rise in over three years
🟣 Your benefits are not cut. PIP, DLA, Attendance Allowance, ESA, Carer's Allowance and Universal Credit are not linked to interest rates
🏠 Tracker and variable mortgages, credit cards and overdrafts may cost more, and everyday prices are still rising for a while
💰 Universal Credit and savings: under £6,000 is not counted, £6,000 to £16,000 reduces your payment, over £16,000 usually means no UC
✅ What to do now: check what you can claim, cut your bills, avoid using credit for essentials, and get free debt advice
