Obesity is not a personal failing 💜

📊 Around 1 in 4 adults in England live with obesity. It is a long-term health condition
🧬 Many causes: genes, some medicines, health conditions, sleep and stress, money and time
📏 As a guide, try to keep your waist to less than half your height
⬇️ Losing even 5% of your body weight can improve your health
🩺 Ask your GP: "I would like some support with my weight. What is available to me?"
