Attendance Allowance explained 💜

👵 For people over State Pension age with an illness or disability who need help with personal care, or someone to keep them safe
🛁 Personal care includes washing, dressing, using the toilet, eating, taking medicines and getting in and out of bed
✅ It is about the help you need, not whether anyone actually helps you
💷 £114.60 a week at the higher rate, or £76.70 at the lower rate (from April 2026). Your income and savings do not matter
➕ It can also mean more Pension Credit, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction and Carer's Allowance for your carer
