Drug dependence: no judgement 💜

🔍 Signs: feeling you cannot cope without it, needing more, withdrawal when you stop, finding it hard to stop
🛡️ Staying safer: do not use alone, avoid mixing (including with alcohol), carry naloxone and use clean equipment
📉 Tolerance drops quickly after a break, such as prison, hospital or detox, which raises the risk of overdose
🚑 Overdose: call 999 if someone is unconscious, not breathing properly or has blue or grey lips. Recovery position, naloxone if you have it, and stay with them
📞 Free, confidential help: local drug services (you can refer yourself), your GP, or FRANK on 0300 123 6600

General information, not medical advice. It is a health condition, and people recover every day.
