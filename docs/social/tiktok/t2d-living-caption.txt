Living with type 2 diabetes 💙

🥗 Fill half your plate with veg, choose wholegrains, cut down on sugary drinks and salt, and include beans and fish
🚶 Walking counts. Sit less and build up to 150 minutes of activity a week
🦶 Check your feet every day. See a GP or podiatrist the same day about any cut or sore that is not healing
🚗 If you are treated with insulin, you must tell the DVLA
💭 Feeling worried or worn out is common. Talk to your GP or diabetes nurse
