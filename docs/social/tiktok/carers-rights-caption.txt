Caring for someone? Know your rights 💜

📋 A free carer's assessment from the council of the person you care for. It can lead to breaks, help at home, training or a personal budget
🗓️ 1 week of unpaid carer's leave every 12 months, from your first day in a job
🏡 All employees can ask for flexible working from day one
💉 Carers can get a free flu jab. Tell your GP you are a carer
🧒 Young carers have the right to a young carer's needs assessment too

Read about carers' rights: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not legal advice.
