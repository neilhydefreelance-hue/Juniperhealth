Mesalazine: for colitis 💊

🩺 It reduces inflammation in the bowel and is the main treatment for mild to moderate ulcerative colitis
🔁 Stay on the same brand, as different brands release the medicine in different parts of the bowel
🤢 Common side effects: headache, feeling sick, stomach pain, diarrhoea and rash
🩸 You need regular blood tests to check your kidneys and blood count
🚨 Get help for unexplained bruising or a sore throat, worsening bloody diarrhoea with a fever, or chest pain or breathlessness

Read about mesalazine: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. Stopping can lead to a flare-up, so talk to your IBD team first.
