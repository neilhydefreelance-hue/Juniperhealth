What is gout? Explained simply 💜

💎 Too much uric acid forms sharp crystals in and around the joints
📊 Around 1 in 40 adults in the UK have gout, and it is more common in men
🦶 An attack comes on quickly, often at night. The joint, usually the big toe, becomes hot, red, swollen and very painful
🍺 Risks include family history, being overweight, kidney disease, water tablets, beer, spirits and sugary drinks
💊 Anti-inflammatories, colchicine and steroids ease flares. Allopurinol helps stop them
🌡️ A painful swollen joint with a high temperature needs same day help
