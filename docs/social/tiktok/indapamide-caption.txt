Indapamide: what to know 💊

🩺 A water tablet that lowers blood pressure, by helping the kidneys remove salt and water and relaxing blood vessels
😣 Common side effects: low potassium, peeing more, headache, dizziness and skin rash
🩸 You need regular blood tests to check your potassium and sodium levels
⚠️ Call 111 or your doctor for muscle cramps, weakness, confusion, an irregular heartbeat, or fainting
🦶 It can make gout worse

Read about indapamide: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. If you stop, your blood pressure will rise again.
