Life after a stroke 💜

😴 Tiredness after a stroke is very common. Plan rest, pace yourself and build up slowly
🗣️ With aphasia, take your time and use gestures, pictures or writing
💭 Low mood, anxiety, anger and crying easily are common. Tell your GP or stroke team
🚗 Do not drive for 1 month after a stroke or TIA. Still have problems after that? Tell the DVLA
🏠 Your council can assess your care needs, and family carers can ask for a carer's assessment

Read about stroke: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. The Stroke Association has a free helpline. Think FAST and call 999 if you see signs of a stroke.
