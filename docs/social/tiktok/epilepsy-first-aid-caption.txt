Seizure first aid: could you help? 💜

🧠 Around 630,000 people in the UK have epilepsy
⏱️ Stay calm and note the time
🛟 Move danger away and cushion their head
🚫 Do not hold them down or put anything in their mouth
🔄 After the jerking stops, put them in the recovery position
🚨 Call 999 if it lasts more than 5 minutes, one seizure follows another, they are injured, it is their first seizure, or they do not recover
