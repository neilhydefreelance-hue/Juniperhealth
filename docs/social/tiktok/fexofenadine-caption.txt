Fexofenadine: a non-drowsy antihistamine 🌼

🤧 It treats hay fever, itchy eyes and nose, and hives
🍊 Do not take it with orange, apple or grapefruit juice, as it makes it work less well
⏰ Leave 2 hours between it and indigestion remedies containing aluminium or magnesium
🤕 Common side effects: headache, dizziness, feeling sick and tiredness
🩺 Get help if you have a fast or irregular heartbeat
