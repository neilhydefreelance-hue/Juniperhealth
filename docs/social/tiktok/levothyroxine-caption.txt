Levothyroxine: what to know 💊

🦋 It replaces the thyroid hormone your body is not making enough of. Most people with an underactive thyroid take it for life
☕ Take it on an empty stomach, usually 30 to 60 minutes before breakfast or caffeine
⏰ Leave 4 hours between it and iron, calcium or indigestion remedies
💓 Dose too high? Look out for a fast heartbeat, sweating, shaking, feeling anxious or trouble sleeping
🩸 You need blood tests to check your dose, especially when starting or if you become pregnant
