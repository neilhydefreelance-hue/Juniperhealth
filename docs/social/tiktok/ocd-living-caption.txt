Living with OCD 🧠

💭 Thoughts are not facts. Notice a thought, label it "that is OCD" and let it pass
⏳ Try delaying a compulsion by a few minutes, or doing it a little less. Work on this with your therapist if you can
🔁 Asking others or searching online for reassurance brings short relief but feeds OCD
😴 Stress, tiredness and low mood can make OCD worse. Sleep, activity and things you enjoy all help
👪 OCD-UK and OCD Action have information for families and carers, and run support groups

Read about OCD: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
