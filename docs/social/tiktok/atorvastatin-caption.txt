Atorvastatin: statins explained 💊

❤️ It lowers cholesterol and reduces your risk of a heart attack or stroke
🤕 Common side effects: headache, muscle aches, feeling sick and indigestion
💪 Muscle pain? Tell your doctor. It is often not caused by the statin, but it should be checked
🍊 Avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice. Not for use in pregnancy or breastfeeding
📈 Cholesterol rises again within weeks if you stop

General information, not medical advice. Get help for unexplained muscle pain with a high temperature or dark pee, or yellow skin or eyes.
