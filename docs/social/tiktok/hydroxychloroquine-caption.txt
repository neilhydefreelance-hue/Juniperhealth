Hydroxychloroquine: what to know 💊

🦴 It treats rheumatoid arthritis, lupus and skin conditions made worse by sunlight
⏳ It takes 2 to 3 months to work fully
👁️ Long-term use can damage the back of the eye. You need regular eye checks after 5 years, or sooner if you are at higher risk
⚠️ Blurring or difficulty reading? Stop and contact your doctor
🧒 Keep it away from children. Even a small number of tablets can be dangerous
