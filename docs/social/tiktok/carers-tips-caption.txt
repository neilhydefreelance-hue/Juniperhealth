Are you a carer? You may not call yourself one 💜

🤝 If you regularly help someone who could not manage without you, you have rights and may get support
🩺 Tell your GP. Many surgeries offer flexible appointments, health checks and a free flu vaccine for carers
📋 Ask your council for a free carer's assessment
💷 Carer's Allowance is money for people who care for 35 hours a week or more
🎟️ Many venues give free carer tickets, and Carers UK runs a free helpline

Read help for carers: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Your council can tell you more.
