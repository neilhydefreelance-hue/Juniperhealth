What is osteoporosis? Explained simply 💜

🦴 Osteoporosis makes bones thinner and weaker, so they break more easily
📊 More than 3.5 million people in the UK have it
🤫 It usually has no symptoms until a bone breaks, often the wrist, hip or spine
⚠️ Risks include the menopause, a parent who broke a hip, long-term steroid tablets and some conditions
🩻 A quick, painless DEXA scan measures bone density
💊 Tablets, injections, HRT, calcium and vitamin D can help
