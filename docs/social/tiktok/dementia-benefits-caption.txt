Dementia: help you can claim 💜

👵 Over State Pension age? Look at Attendance Allowance. The higher rate is for help or supervision both day and night
🧑 Under State Pension age? PIP can help. Dementia can affect almost every PIP activity
🏠 A person with dementia may be disregarded for council tax, which can mean a discount or no bill
🤝 Carer's Allowance, for a family member who cares for at least 35 hours a week
💼 Still working? Dementia counts as a disability at work. Get advice before leaving a job

Read about dementia help: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not legal advice. Citizens Advice or a local welfare rights service can help for free.
