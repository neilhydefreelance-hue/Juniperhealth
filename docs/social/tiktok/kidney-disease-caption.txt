Chronic kidney disease explained 💜

🫘 Around 1 in 10 adults in the UK have chronic kidney disease (CKD), and many do not know
🔍 Early CKD usually has no symptoms, so it is found through blood and urine tests
🩸 Common causes include diabetes, high blood pressure and heart disease
✅ If you have diabetes, high blood pressure or heart disease, get your kidneys checked regularly
💊 Blood pressure medicines, SGLT2 inhibitors, statins and healthy changes can slow it down
