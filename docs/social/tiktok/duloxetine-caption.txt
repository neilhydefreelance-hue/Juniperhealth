Duloxetine: for mood and pain 💊

🧠 It treats depression, generalised anxiety, diabetic nerve pain and stress incontinence
🦴 It is also used for fibromyalgia, long-term back pain and osteoarthritis pain
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, dry mouth, headache, dizziness and sweating
🍷 It can raise blood pressure. No St John's wort, and avoid lots of alcohol
🛑 Do not stop suddenly. Withdrawal can be severe, so your doctor will lower the dose over at least 2 to 4 weeks
