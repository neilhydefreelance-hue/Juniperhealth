Disabled Facilities Grant: help to adapt your home 🏠

🛠️ It can pay for stairlifts, a level-access shower, ramps, wider doors or a downstairs bathroom
💷 Up to £30,000 in England. In Wales, the limit is higher
👧 Adults may pay towards it, but there is no means test for disabled children under 18
📞 Contact your council. An occupational therapist will usually assess what you need
⚠️ Do not start work before the grant is approved, or you may not be paid
