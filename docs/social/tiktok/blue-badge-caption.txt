Blue Badge explained 💜

🅿️ You usually get one automatically with DLA higher rate mobility, 8+ points in PIP moving around, or if you are severely sight impaired
🧠 Hidden disabilities count, such as autism, dementia or a mental health condition, if journeys cause very considerable difficulty or overwhelming distress
⏱️ You can usually park on single or double yellow lines for up to 3 hours, unless there is a loading ban
💷 Up to £10 in England, free in Wales. A badge usually lasts up to 3 years
