Naltrexone: staying off alcohol or opioids 💜

🧠 It blocks opioids and reduces the rewarding effect of alcohol, which helps lower cravings
⏳ You must be completely off opioids for 7 to 10 days before starting, or it causes sudden, severe withdrawal
🪪 Carry a card saying you take it, as normal opioid painkillers will not work
⚠️ Never try to overcome the block with large amounts of opioids. After stopping, your tolerance is very low and overdose can be fatal
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, headache, trouble sleeping, feeling restless and joint pain
