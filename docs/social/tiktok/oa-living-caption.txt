Living with osteoarthritis 🦵

🏊 Keep moving every day. Swimming, cycling, walking and tai chi are kind to joints
⏸️ Pace yourself. Break big jobs into smaller chunks, and try not to overdo it on a good day
🔥 Flare-up? Use heat or cold, keep gently moving, do less for a few days, and use pain relief as advised
🦯 Handy aids: a walking stick, jar openers, a raised toilet seat and a long-handled grabber
📞 Your council can arrange a free needs assessment. Versus Arthritis helpline: 0800 5200 520

Read about osteoarthritis: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. See your GP if a flare-up does not settle.
