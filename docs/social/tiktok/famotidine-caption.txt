Famotidine: what to know 💊

🔥 It reduces stomach acid, for heartburn, acid reflux and stomach ulcers
🧪 It blocks histamine on the stomach lining, which normally tells the stomach to make acid
😣 Common side effects: headache, dizziness, and constipation or diarrhoea
⚠️ Call 111 or your doctor if you get a fast or irregular heartbeat, confusion, or yellow skin or eyes
🫘 People with kidney problems may need a lower dose
