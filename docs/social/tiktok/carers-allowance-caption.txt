Carer's Allowance explained 💜

💷 £86.45 a week from April 2026, if you care for someone for at least 35 hours a week
✅ You must be 16 or over, not in full-time education, and earn £204 a week or less
🧾 The person you care for needs a qualifying benefit, such as PIP daily living, DLA care (middle or highest rate) or Attendance Allowance
🏠 They do not have to be family or live with you
🛡️ It gives you National Insurance credits to protect your State Pension, and can be backdated up to 3 months
🤝 Also ask for a free carer's assessment and tell your GP you are a carer
