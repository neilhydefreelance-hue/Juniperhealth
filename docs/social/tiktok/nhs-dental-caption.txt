Free or cheaper NHS dental care 🦷

✅ Free if you are under 18, pregnant or had a baby in the last 12 months, get certain benefits, or have an HC2 certificate
💷 England has three price bands: Band 1 check-ups, Band 2 fillings and root canal, Band 3 crowns and dentures
1️⃣ You pay one charge for each course of treatment, however many visits it takes
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 In Wales, check-ups are free if you are under 25 or 60 or over
📞 No NHS dentist? Search the NHS website, or call NHS 111 for urgent dental care
