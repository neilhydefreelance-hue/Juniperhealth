Free help on trains and planes ✈️🚆

🚆 Passenger Assist gives free help on trains to get on and off, with luggage and to change trains. Book up to 2 hours ahead, or turn up and go
✈️ You have the right to free help at airports, including for hidden disabilities. Tell your airline at least 48 hours before you fly
🌻 A Sunflower lanyard shows you may need extra help, time or patience
🚌 National Express offers a Disabled Coachcard with money off coach fares
🚕 Taxis must not refuse assistance dogs, and wheelchair users must not be charged extra in accessible taxis
