Dapagliflozin: what to know 💊

❤️ It treats type 2 diabetes, heart failure and chronic kidney disease, by helping you pass extra sugar in your pee
😣 Common side effects: thrush, urine infections, peeing more, thirst and dizziness
🤒 Sick day rules: stop and get advice if you are unwell and cannot eat or drink, or before major surgery
🚨 Feeling or being sick, stomach pain, fast breathing or sweet-smelling breath can be signs of DKA. Get help fast
💧 Drink plenty of fluids, and keep your genital area clean to lower the risk of infections
