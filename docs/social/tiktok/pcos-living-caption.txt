Living with PCOS 🌸

🥗 There is no special PCOS diet. Vegetables, wholegrains, beans and lean protein can help your body manage insulin
🏋️ Aim for at least 150 minutes of activity a week. Strength exercises can be especially helpful
💭 Anxiety and depression are more common in PCOS. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies
🩺 Ask your GP about regular checks for blood sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol
📅 See your GP if you have fewer than 3 or 4 periods a year, or any unusual bleeding
