Help with energy and water bills 💜

⚡ Warm Home Discount: £150 off your electricity bill each winter, usually automatic with a means-tested benefit
📋 Priority Services Register: free power cut warnings, accessible bills and a password scheme
💧 WaterSure caps your water bill if you have a meter, get a means-tested benefit and use a lot of water for medical reasons
❄️ Cold Weather Payments: £25 for each 7 day spell of very cold weather, paid automatically with certain benefits
🔌 Use a dialysis machine or oxygen at home? The NHS may refund the electricity
