What is ADHD? Explained simply 💜

🧠 ADHD affects how the brain manages attention, activity and impulses. It is not caused by bad parenting or lack of effort
🔍 Inattention: easily distracted, forgetting and losing things, not finishing tasks, finding planning hard
⚡ Hyperactivity and impulsiveness: restlessness, talking a lot, acting without thinking, finding it hard to wait
👩 It is often missed in adults, especially women, who may have mainly inattentive symptoms
🩺 See your GP for a referral. In England, ask about Right to Choose for a possibly shorter wait
