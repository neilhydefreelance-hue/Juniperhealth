Rizatriptan (Maxalt): migraine medicine 💊

⏱️ It works best when taken as soon as the headache starts. It comes as tablets or wafers that melt on the tongue
😴 Common side effects: feeling sleepy or dizzy, tingling, a heavy or tight chest, and dry mouth
💊 Taking propranolol? You need a lower dose of rizatriptan
📅 Taking triptans on 10 or more days a month can cause medication overuse headaches. Your GP can help
🚑 Call 999 for chest pain or tightness that is severe or does not go away

General information, not medical advice. Not suitable if you have heart disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure or have had a stroke.
