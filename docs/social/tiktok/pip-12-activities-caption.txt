The 12 PIP activities, and the most points each one can score 👇

Daily living
1. Preparing food (up to 8)
2. Eating and drinking (up to 10)
3. Managing medicines and treatment (up to 8)
4. Washing and bathing (up to 8)
5. Using the toilet (up to 8)
6. Dressing (up to 8)
7. Talking and listening (up to 12)
8. Reading (up to 8)
9. Mixing with people (up to 8)
10. Managing money (up to 6)

Mobility
11. Planning and following journeys (up to 12)
12. Moving around (up to 12)

8 points in a part gets the standard rate. 12 gets the enhanced rate. The statement that fits you on most days is the one that counts.
