What is PCOS? Explained simply 💜

🌸 Polycystic ovary syndrome affects how the ovaries work
📊 It is thought to affect around 1 in 10 women and people with ovaries in the UK, and many do not know they have it
📝 Common symptoms: irregular periods, extra hair, thinning hair, acne, weight gain and trouble getting pregnant
🩺 It is usually diagnosed if you have 2 of 3 signs: irregular periods, signs of higher testosterone, or polycystic ovaries on a scan
💪 There is no cure, but being active, healthy eating, the pill, metformin and other treatments can help
