How to fill in your PIP form 📝

📅 You have one month to send it back. Need more time? Ring the PIP enquiry line before the deadline
😣 Describe a bad day, and say how many bad days you have in a typical week
✅ You only count as able to do something if you can do it safely, to an acceptable standard, again and again, and in a reasonable time
✍️ Give real examples: how long things take, who helps you, and what happens if you try
📸 Photo every page, and ask the Post Office for free proof of postage
