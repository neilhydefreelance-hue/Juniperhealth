Living with hearing loss 👂

🗣️ Ask people to face you, speak clearly, get your attention first, rephrase rather than repeat, and turn off the TV
📱 Relay UK is a free service that lets you use text to make and take phone calls
🚨 Register for emergencySMS and you can text 999 in an emergency
🔔 Your local fire service may fit flashing or vibrating smoke alarms for free
🤟 You have the right to request a BSL interpreter for NHS appointments

Read about hearing loss: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. RNID's free information line can offer support.
