Anxiety: self-help tips 🌿

🌬️ Calm breathing: in through your nose for 4, out through your mouth for 6, and let your shoulders drop
📝 Worry time: set 15 minutes a day to write worries down, and save other worries for later
☕ Caffeine can trigger anxiety, and alcohol makes it worse the next day
🚶 A daily walk helps burn off the physical effects of anxiety and helps you sleep
🪜 Avoiding things can make anxiety grow. Try facing them in small, manageable steps

General information, not medical advice. Anxiety UK and Mind offer helplines. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
