What is costochondritis? Explained simply 💜

🦴 It is inflammation of the cartilage that joins your ribs to your breastbone
😣 It causes sharp or aching chest pain, often on the left, that gets worse when you breathe in or move
🚨 Chest pain can be serious. Call 999 if it feels tight, heavy or crushing, spreads to your arms, neck, jaw or back, or you are short of breath, sweaty or dizzy
💜 Costochondritis is not dangerous, and most people are better within 1 to 2 months
🔥 Painkillers, heat or ice, rest from heavy lifting and gentle stretches can help
