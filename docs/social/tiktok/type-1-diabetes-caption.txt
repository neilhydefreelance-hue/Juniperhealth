Type 1 diabetes: know the 4 Ts 💜

🛡️ In type 1 diabetes, the immune system destroys the cells that make insulin. It is not caused by diet or lifestyle
🚽 Toilet: peeing more, or bedwetting in a child who was dry
💧 Thirsty: very thirsty
😴 Tired: very tired
⚖️ Thinner: losing weight without trying
🩺 Spot the 4 Ts? See a GP the same day or call NHS 111
🚨 Call 999 if there is also being sick, tummy pain, fruity breath, fast breathing or drowsiness
