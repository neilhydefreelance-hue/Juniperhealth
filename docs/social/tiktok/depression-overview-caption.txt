What is depression? Explained simply 💜

🌧️ Depression means feeling persistently low for weeks or months, in a way that affects daily life
📊 Around 1 in 6 adults in the UK have a common mental health problem such as depression in any given week
📝 Signs include feeling hopeless, losing interest, tiredness, sleep changes and feeling worthless
🩺 Talk to your GP, or in England refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies
💬 Self-help, CBT, group exercise, counselling and antidepressants can all help
