Symbicort and combination inhalers explained 🫁

💨 A steroid preventer (budesonide) with a long-acting reliever (formoterol), for asthma and COPD
📋 Some people use it as both their preventer and their reliever. Your asthma action plan should say exactly how to use it
🪥 Rinse your mouth after each use
🤕 Common side effects: hoarse voice, oral thrush, headache, shaking and a fast heartbeat
🩺 Get medical help if you need more than your maximum number of puffs in a day, or wheezing gets worse straight after using it

Read about this inhaler: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. In an asthma attack, call 999 if your inhaler is not helping.
