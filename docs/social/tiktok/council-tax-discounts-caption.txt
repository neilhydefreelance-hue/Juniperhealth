Council tax discounts you might be missing 💜

🏠 Disabled Band Reduction: if your home has an extra bathroom, a room used for your needs, or space for a wheelchair, your bill may drop a band
💷 It does not depend on income
🧠 Severe mental impairment, such as dementia: 25% off, or the home may be exempt if they live alone
🤝 Some live-in carers can be disregarded, and low incomes can get Council Tax Reduction
⏪ Many councils will backdate discounts, sometimes by several years
