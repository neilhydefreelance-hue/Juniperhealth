Carbocisteine: what to know 💊

🫁 It makes mucus thinner and easier to cough up, and can mean fewer flare-ups in COPD
😣 Common side effects: feeling sick, stomach discomfort and diarrhoea
⚠️ Get help straight away if you are sick with blood, or have black or bloody poo. Call 111 or your doctor
🚫 Not suitable if you have an active stomach ulcer, or in the first 3 months of pregnancy
📅 Your doctor may review whether it is helping after about 4 weeks
