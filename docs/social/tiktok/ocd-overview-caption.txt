What is OCD? Explained simply 💜

💭 Obsessions are unwanted thoughts, images or urges that keep coming back and cause distress
🧼 Compulsions are things you feel you have to do, like washing, checking, counting or asking for reassurance
🔁 Compulsions bring short-term relief but keep the cycle going
💜 Intrusive thoughts are a symptom of OCD. Health professionals understand
💬 CBT with exposure and response prevention can help. In England, refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies
