What is coeliac disease? 💜

🌾 Coeliac disease is an autoimmune condition, not a food intolerance
🛡️ Gluten, found in wheat, barley and rye, makes the immune system attack the lining of the gut
📊 Around 1 in 100 people in the UK have it, but most have not been diagnosed
😣 Symptoms include diarrhoea, tummy pain, bloating, tiredness or anaemia, weight loss and an itchy rash
🩸 Keep eating gluten until testing is finished, as the blood test only works if you do
🍞 Treatment is a lifelong gluten free diet, with dietitian support
