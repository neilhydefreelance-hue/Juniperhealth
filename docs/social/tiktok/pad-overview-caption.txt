Legs hurt when you walk? It could be PAD 💜

🦵 Peripheral arterial disease (PAD) is when narrowed arteries reduce blood flow to your legs
😣 Cramping in the calves, thighs or buttocks when walking that eases with rest is called intermittent claudication
🦶 Other signs: cold or numb feet, hair loss on the legs, shiny pale skin, and sores that do not heal
🚭 Smoking is the biggest risk. Diabetes, high blood pressure and high cholesterol raise it too
🚑 Call 999 if your leg or foot suddenly becomes painful, cold, pale, blue or numb, or you cannot move it
