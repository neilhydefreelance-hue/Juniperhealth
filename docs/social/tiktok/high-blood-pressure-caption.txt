High blood pressure: the silent condition 💜

🤫 High blood pressure rarely causes symptoms. The only way to know is to get it checked
📊 140/90 or higher (135/85 at home) usually means high blood pressure. Healthy is 90/60 to 120/80
❤️ It raises the risk of heart attacks, strokes, kidney disease, vascular dementia and sight problems
🧂 Risks include age, too much salt, being overweight, alcohol, smoking and family history
🥗 Less than 6g of salt a day, more fruit and veg, 150 active minutes a week and less alcohol can help
