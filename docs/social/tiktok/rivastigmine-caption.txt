Rivastigmine: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats the symptoms of Alzheimer's disease and dementia in Parkinson's disease
🩹 It comes as capsules, liquid or a daily skin patch
😣 Common side effects: feeling or being sick, less appetite, weight loss, dizziness and diarrhoea
⚠️ Take off the old patch before putting on a new one. More than one patch can cause a dangerous overdose
🔁 Missed more than 3 days? It must be restarted at the lowest dose. Ask your doctor
