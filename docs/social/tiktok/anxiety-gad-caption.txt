What is generalised anxiety disorder? 💜

😟 GAD means feeling anxious most days, about lots of different things
📊 The NHS estimates it affects up to 5 in 100 people in the UK
💓 It can cause constant worry, a fast heartbeat, muscle tension, an upset stomach and trouble sleeping
🩺 In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies without seeing a GP
💬 Self-help, CBT, applied relaxation and medicine such as sertraline can help
