Valproate: what to know 💊

🧠 A very effective medicine for epilepsy, also used for bipolar disorder
🤰 It can seriously harm an unborn baby. Women and girls who could get pregnant must be on the Pregnancy Prevention Programme and use highly effective contraception
👨 Men starting valproate are now usually reviewed by two specialists
😣 Common side effects: feeling sick, weight gain, hair thinning, shaking, stomach pain and sleepiness
🛑 Never stop it on your own, even if you find out you are pregnant. Speak to your doctor urgently instead
