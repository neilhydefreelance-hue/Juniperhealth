Aids that help with COPD 🫁

🏥 Free from your NHS team: pulmonary rehab, a self-management plan, help with airway clearance, and oxygen if tests show you need it
🏠 Equipment for daily living under £1,000 should be free if your council says you need it
🌬️ A handheld fan held about 15cm from your face can ease breathlessness, alongside your breathing techniques
🪑 Save energy: sit to shower, use a perching stool, a grabber and a trolley
🚑 Call 999 if you are struggling to breathe, have chest pain, or your lips or skin look blue or grey

General information, not medical advice. No aid replaces your inhalers, rescue pack or treatment plan.
