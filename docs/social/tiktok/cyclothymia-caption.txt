Bipolar explained, part 3 of 3: what is cyclothymia? 💜

🔄 Cyclothymia causes ongoing mild highs and lows that are not severe enough, or long enough, to be diagnosed as bipolar disorder
📅 For a diagnosis, the mood changes usually go on for at least 2 years in adults, or 1 year in children and teenagers
💥 It can still have a big effect on relationships, work and studies
📓 A mood diary, talking therapies, steady sleep and routines can help. Some people take a mood stabiliser
🩺 For some people it can develop into bipolar disorder, so keep in touch with your GP
