How to get the Universal Credit health element 📋

📢 Tell the DWP: report a change of circumstances in your online account, or say you have a health condition when you apply
📝 Send a fit note to your journal. The 3 month waiting period usually starts from the date you give medical evidence
✍️ Fill in the UC50 within about 4 weeks. Describe your worst days and add letters from your care team
🩺 Most people then have a Work Capability Assessment by phone, video or face to face
⏩ Terminally ill? Ask for an SR1 form. No assessment and no waiting period
