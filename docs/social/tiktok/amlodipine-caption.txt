Amlodipine: what to know 💊

🩺 A common medicine for high blood pressure and preventing angina
🫀 It relaxes and widens your blood vessels, so blood flows more easily and blood pressure falls
🦶 Swollen ankles are common. Tell your doctor if this bothers you
🤕 Other side effects: headache, flushing, dizziness, tiredness and palpitations
🍊 Avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice
