Coronary heart disease: know the signs 💜

❤️ Fatty deposits narrow the arteries that carry blood to your heart muscle
😣 Angina is a tight, heavy chest pain often brought on by activity, cold or stress, which eases with rest
🚨 Heart attack signs: chest pain that does not go away, pain spreading to your arm, jaw or back, sweating, feeling sick or breathless. Call 999
👩 Women, older people and people with diabetes may have milder symptoms. If in doubt, call 999
🚭 Risks you can change include smoking, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, inactivity and alcohol
