Hay fever: cold or pollen? 🌼

🤧 Hay fever is an allergy to pollen and affects around 1 in 4 people in the UK
👀 It lasts weeks or months, often with itchy eyes, and does not cause a high temperature
🌳 Trees: late March to mid May. Grass: mid May to July. Weeds: end of June to September
💊 Non-drowsy antihistamines, steroid nose sprays and eye drops can help. Ask a pharmacist
📅 Start steroid nose sprays 2 weeks before your usual season and use them every day
