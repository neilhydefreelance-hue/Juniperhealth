What is changing for disabled people? 📢

💷 Since April 2026, most new claimants get the lower Universal Credit health element of £217.26 a month. Others, including people who are terminally ill, get £429.80
🛡️ Already getting it? You keep the protected higher rate while you still qualify
📝 Planned but not yet law: ending the Work Capability Assessment, linking health money to PIP, and changes for under 22s
🔍 The Timms review of PIP is still under way. No PIP rules have changed because of it yet
✅ What to do now: claim if you qualify, check if you could get PIP, and get free advice
