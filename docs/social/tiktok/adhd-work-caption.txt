ADHD at work: your rights 💜

⚖️ ADHD often counts as a disability if it has a substantial, long-term effect on daily life. You do not always need a formal diagnosis to ask for support
🛠️ Reasonable adjustments: written instructions, a quieter workspace, regular check-ins, flexible hours and planning software
🤝 Access to Work can pay for coaching, equipment and software
📋 PIP can help if ADHD means you forget food on the hob, need medicine reminders, need help with money or get lost on journeys
🧒 Children with ADHD may get DLA if they need much more supervision than other children their age

Read about ADHD and work: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not legal advice. Tell the DVLA if your ADHD affects your ability to drive safely.
