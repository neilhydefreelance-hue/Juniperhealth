Could you get free NHS prescriptions? 💜

💊 Lots of people can get free or cheaper prescriptions, dental treatment, sight tests and glasses, but do not know it
✅ You may get help because of your age, pregnancy or a new baby, some health conditions, some benefits or a low income
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 In Wales, prescriptions are free for everyone registered with a GP
🧾 The NHS Low Income Scheme can help with prescriptions, dental care, sight tests and travel to hospital
⚠️ Check before you tick the box. Claiming when you are not entitled can mean a penalty of up to £100
