Crohn's and colitis explained 💜

🔥 Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) is a lifelong condition where parts of the gut become inflamed
📊 Around 1 in 123 people in the UK have IBD. It often starts between 15 and 40
🚽 Symptoms include diarrhoea (sometimes with blood), urgent need for the toilet, tummy pain, extreme tiredness and weight loss
💊 Mesalazine, steroids for flares, immunosuppressants, biologics and surgery can help
🚨 Go to A&E for severe tummy pain, a swollen tummy, heavy bleeding or if you cannot keep fluids down
