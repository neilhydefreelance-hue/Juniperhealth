Heartburn and acid reflux explained 💜

🔥 Acid reflux is when stomach acid comes back up into your food pipe. It is very common
😖 Symptoms include burning in the chest, a sour taste, bringing food up, a cough and a hoarse voice
☕ Triggers include coffee, alcohol, spicy or fatty food, smoking, being overweight and stress
💊 Antacids, Gaviscon, omeprazole from your GP and healthy changes can help
⚠️ See a GP if you have difficulty swallowing, weight loss without trying, vomiting blood or black poo
