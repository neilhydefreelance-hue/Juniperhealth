What is COPD? Explained simply 💜

🫁 COPD is a group of lung conditions, including emphysema and chronic bronchitis, that make it hard to breathe out
😮‍💨 Symptoms: breathlessness, a chesty cough, chest infections, wheezing and tiredness
🚬 It is not just a smoker's cough. Early signs are often missed, so ask about a spirometry test
💪 Stopping smoking, pulmonary rehab, inhalers, vaccines and a rescue pack can help
🚨 Call 999 if you are severely breathless and treatment is not helping, have chest pain, blue or grey lips, or are confused or drowsy
