Tingling fingers? It could be carpal tunnel 💜

✋ Carpal tunnel syndrome happens when a nerve in your wrist gets squeezed
📌 Signs include pins and needles, numbness, symptoms worse at night, a weak grip and dropping things
⚠️ It is more likely with pregnancy, diabetes, an underactive thyroid, rheumatoid arthritis or using vibrating tools
🩹 Wrist splints at night, changing how you use your hands, steroid injections and surgery can help
