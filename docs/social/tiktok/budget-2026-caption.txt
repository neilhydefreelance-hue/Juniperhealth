The Autumn Budget: what it means for disabled people 📢

🟣 PIP, DLA and Attendance Allowance: no changes in the Budget. Decisions on PIP wait for the government's response to the Timms Review
💷 A one-off £100 Household Energy Payment for homes in council tax bands A to D in England, expected from January 2027
🔥 Plans to remove the separate standing charge on gas bills from 2027. No confirmed change for electricity
🤝 Carer's Allowance: the earnings cliff edge will be replaced with a taper, but not until 2030 to 2031
✅ What to do now: check your council tax band, check what you can claim, watch out for scams and get free advice
