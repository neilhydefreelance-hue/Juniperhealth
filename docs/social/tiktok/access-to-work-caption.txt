Access to Work explained 💜

💼 Grants for people with a disability, health condition or mental health condition that makes parts of work harder
🪑 It can pay for equipment, workplace adaptations, taxis to work, a support worker or a BSL interpreter
🧠 The Mental Health Support Service gives free, confidential support for 9 months, with no diagnosis needed
✅ Apply early, explain your barriers with examples, and keep records

Read our free work and study guide: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information. Apply online on GOV.UK or by phone.
