Lamotrigine: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats epilepsy and helps prevent episodes of depression in bipolar disorder
😴 Common side effects: headache, skin rash, dizziness, feeling sick, tiredness and blurred vision
⚠️ Get help straight away for any skin rash, especially with a high temperature, sore mouth, blisters or peeling
🐢 The dose goes up slowly at the start, to lower the risk of a serious rash
🛑 Never stop suddenly. If you miss more than 5 days, do not restart at your usual dose. Ask your doctor
