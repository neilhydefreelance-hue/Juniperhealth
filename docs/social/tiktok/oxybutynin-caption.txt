Oxybutynin: overactive bladder medicine 💊

🚻 It relaxes the bladder, so you can hold more urine and feel less urgency
👄 Common side effects: dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision, feeling dizzy or sleepy, and dry eyes
🧓 In older people it may affect memory and thinking. Long-term use of this type of medicine has been linked to a higher risk of dementia. Ask about other options
🌡️ It reduces sweating, so avoid getting too hot
🩺 Get help if you cannot pee, have confusion or see things, or have eye pain with blurred vision
