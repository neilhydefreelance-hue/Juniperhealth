Alcohol dependence: no judgement 💜

🍷 Signs include a strong urge to drink, needing more for the same effect, drinking in the morning, and shaking or sweating when you stop
⚠️ If you drink heavily every day, do not stop suddenly. It can cause fits. Talk to your GP or local alcohol service first
🚑 Call 999 if someone has a fit, is very confused, or is seeing things after stopping drinking
📏 The UK guide is no more than 14 units a week, spread over 3 or more days
📞 Free, confidential help: your GP, local alcohol services (you can refer yourself) or Drinkline on 0300 123 1110
