Bipolar medicines, part 1 of 2: mood stabilisers 💊💜

1️⃣ Lithium: usually the first choice long term. You need regular blood tests. Get urgent help for sickness, diarrhoea, a bad tremor, unsteadiness or confusion
2️⃣ Lamotrigine: especially helpful for preventing depression. The dose goes up slowly. See a doctor straight away if you get a rash
3️⃣ Valproate: very effective, but it can harm an unborn baby and may affect male fertility. Not usually started in anyone under 55 unless two specialists agree
4️⃣ Carbamazepine: sometimes used when other medicines have not worked. It affects many other medicines, including some contraception

🛑 Never stop a mood stabiliser suddenly. It can bring on mania or depression
