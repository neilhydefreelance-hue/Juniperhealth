Type 1 diabetes: work and benefits 💙

⚖️ It usually counts as a disability at work, because the law looks at how you would be without insulin
⏸️ Ask for adjustments, like breaks to check glucose or treat a hypo, a private place to inject, and time off for appointments
💊 In England, get a medical exemption certificate for free prescriptions. Prescriptions are free in Wales
👧 Many families claim DLA for a child, based on the extra care they need day and night
🧑 Adults may get PIP if they need prompting or supervision with insulin, for example because of frequent hypos, or have complications
