Depression: small steps that help 💜

🌱 Set small, realistic goals, like getting dressed or going outside for 5 minutes. Notice when you manage them
🚶 Even a short walk can lift your mood. Your GP may be able to refer you to an exercise scheme
😴 Keep regular sleep times and some structure to your day
🤝 Talk to people you trust. Alcohol and drugs can make depression worse
🛠️ Free help: NHS Every Mind Matters, Mind, and StepChange or Citizens Advice for money worries

General information, not medical advice. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call 999 if you are in danger, NHS 111 option 2, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time.
