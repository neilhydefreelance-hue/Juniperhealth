Free home equipment and adaptations 💜

🏠 Ask your council's adult social care for a free needs assessment
🛁 In England, equipment and small adaptations under £1,000 are usually free, like grab rails, shower seats and raised toilet seats
🛗 A Disabled Facilities Grant of up to £30,000 in England can pay for stairlifts, level-access showers and ramps
👧 There is no means test for disabled children, and some benefits mean you pay nothing
⚠️ Get the grant approved before any work starts
