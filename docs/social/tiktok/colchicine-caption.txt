Colchicine: for gout attacks 💊

🦶 It treats gout attacks, prevents attacks when starting allopurinol, and is used for pericarditis
⚠️ Never take more than the prescribed dose. Too much colchicine is dangerous
🤢 Common side effects: diarrhoea, feeling or being sick, and stomach pain
🛑 Severe diarrhoea or vomiting? Stop taking it
🍊 Avoid grapefruit. It interacts with some antibiotics, such as clarithromycin, and some statins

General information, not medical advice. Get help for unusual bruising or bleeding, a sore throat or a high temperature.
