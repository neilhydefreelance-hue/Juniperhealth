Amitriptyline for pain? Here is why 💊

🧠 It is an older antidepressant, but at low doses it is now mostly used for nerve pain and to prevent migraines
🩹 It is also used for tension headaches, fibromyalgia, IBS and long-term back pain
🌙 It makes you sleepy, so it is usually taken in the evening. Avoid alcohol
😶 Common side effects: sleepiness, dry mouth, constipation, dizziness and weight gain
⚠️ It can be dangerous in overdose, so keep it safely away from others
