What are cataracts? Explained simply 💜

👁️ Cataracts are when the lens inside your eye becomes cloudy
👵 Most people over 65 have some cloudiness in their lens
🌫️ Signs include blurred or misty sight, faded colours, glare from lights and halos
✅ Cataracts are not painful and do not make your eyes red
👓 An optician can spot them at a routine eye test, and surgery can replace the cloudy lens
