Your blue inhaler: salbutamol 💙

🫁 It relaxes the muscles around your airways within minutes, making breathing easier for 4 to 6 hours
📅 Needing it 3 or more times a week means your asthma is not well controlled. See your GP or nurse
🤲 Common side effects: shaky hands, a pounding heart, headache and muscle cramps
🌀 A spacer helps more of the medicine reach your lungs. Always keep your reliever with you
🚑 In an asthma attack, follow your action plan and call 999 if your reliever is not helping
