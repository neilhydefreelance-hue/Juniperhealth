What is heart failure? Explained simply 💜

❤️ Heart failure means your heart is not pumping blood as well as it should. It does not mean your heart has stopped
📊 Around 1 million people in the UK have heart failure
😮‍💨 Symptoms include breathlessness, tiredness, swollen ankles and sudden weight gain
🩺 It is diagnosed with an NT-proBNP blood test and an echocardiogram
📞 More breathless, more swelling or quick weight gain? Contact your heart failure team or NHS 111
🚨 Call 999 for chest pain, severe breathlessness or coughing up pink, frothy mucus
