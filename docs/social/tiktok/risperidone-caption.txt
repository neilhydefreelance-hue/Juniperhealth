Risperidone: what to know 💊

🧠 An antipsychotic for schizophrenia, mania in bipolar disorder, and short-term aggression in dementia
😴 Common side effects: sleepiness, weight gain, restlessness, stiffness or shaking and headache
🩺 Your weight, blood sugar and cholesterol will be checked regularly
🚨 Call 999 for a high temperature with stiff muscles and confusion, or signs of a stroke in older people with dementia
🛑 Never stop suddenly. Your team will lower the dose slowly
