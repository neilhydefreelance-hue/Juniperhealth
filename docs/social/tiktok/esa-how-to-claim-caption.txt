How to claim New Style ESA 📋

✅ You can claim if you are too ill to work, under State Pension age, have paid enough National Insurance and are not getting Statutory Sick Pay
🗂️ You need your National Insurance number, bank details, GP details and a fit note
🪜 The steps: apply online, send fit notes, fill in the ESA50, have your Work Capability Assessment, then get your decision
💷 On Universal Credit too? You can get both. ESA is not affected by savings and gives you National Insurance credits
⏪ New Style ESA can be backdated for up to 3 months. Ask when you claim
