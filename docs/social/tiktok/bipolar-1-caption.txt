Bipolar explained, part 1 of 3: what is bipolar 1? 💜

🎢 Bipolar 1 means you have had at least one episode of mania. Most people also have episodes of depression
📅 Mania lasts at least a week, or less if hospital treatment is needed
⚡ Signs include a very high or irritable mood, lots of energy, little need for sleep, racing thoughts, grand ideas and risky spending
🧠 Some people have psychosis during mania
💊 Lithium is usually the first choice long term, with antipsychotics for mania and talking therapies
