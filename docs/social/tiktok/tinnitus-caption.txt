Ringing in your ears? It could be tinnitus 💜

🔔 Tinnitus means hearing ringing, buzzing, humming, hissing or whooshing that is not coming from outside
📊 Around 1 in 7 adults in the UK have it
👂 Common causes include hearing loss, loud noise, earwax, ear infections and stress
🎧 Hearing aids, sound therapy, tinnitus counselling and CBT can help
🚨 Call NHS 111 urgently if it comes with sudden hearing loss, beats with your pulse, or comes with dizziness or face numbness
