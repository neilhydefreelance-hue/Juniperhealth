Back and neck pain explained 💜

⏳ Most back pain gets better on its own within 4 to 6 weeks, and it is rarely caused by serious damage
🚶 Staying active is the best thing you can do
🧘 Walking, swimming, stretching, yoga or pilates and physiotherapy can help
🪑 Sitting for long periods, inactivity, stress and poor sleep can make it worse
🚨 Call 999 for back pain with numbness around your bottom or genitals, problems peeing or pooing, or weakness in both legs
