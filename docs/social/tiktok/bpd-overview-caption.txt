Borderline personality disorder explained with care 💜

🧠 BPD (also called EUPD) affects how you manage emotions, see yourself and relate to others
💜 It does not mean your personality is "wrong", and it can be helped
🌊 Signs include intense, changing emotions, fear of abandonment, unstable relationships, feeling empty and impulsive behaviour
🤝 DBT, mentalisation-based therapy and a care and crisis plan can help
🙏 You deserve to be treated with respect and listened to. See your GP to get support
