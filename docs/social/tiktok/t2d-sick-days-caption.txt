Diabetes and being ill: know your sick day rules 🤒

📈 Being unwell can push your blood sugar up
💧 Keep drinking fluids, and eat small amounts if you can
🩸 If you test at home, check your blood sugar more often
⏸️ Some medicines, such as metformin and SGLT2 inhibitors, may need to be paused when you cannot eat or drink
📋 Ask your GP or pharmacist for sick day rules
