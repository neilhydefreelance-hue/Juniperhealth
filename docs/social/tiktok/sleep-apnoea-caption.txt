Sleep apnoea: more than snoring 💜

😴 In obstructive sleep apnoea, your breathing stops and starts while you sleep
📊 More than 1.5 million adults in the UK are thought to have it, and many do not know
🌙 At night: loud snoring, pauses in breathing, gasping or choking
☀️ In the day: feeling very sleepy, poor concentration, morning headaches and low mood
🏠 Your GP can refer you for a sleep study, usually done at home
😷 A CPAP machine, mouth guards and healthy changes can help
