Prednisolone: steroid tablets explained 💊

🫁 Used for asthma attacks, COPD flare-ups, bowel and arthritis flares, severe allergies and more
🍽️ Common side effects: more appetite, trouble sleeping, mood changes and raised blood sugar
⚠️ After more than 3 weeks or high doses, never stop suddenly. It can cause a life-threatening adrenal crisis. The dose must be lowered slowly
🪪 Carry a steroid emergency card if you take it for more than 3 weeks
🩺 Get help if you feel very low, high or confused, have signs of a serious infection, black or bloody poo, or are very thirsty and peeing a lot
