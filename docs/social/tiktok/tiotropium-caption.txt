Tiotropium (Spiriva): once-a-day inhaler 🫁

🌬️ It keeps the airways relaxed and open for 24 hours, easing breathlessness in COPD and some asthma
⚠️ It is not a reliever. Keep using your reliever inhaler for sudden symptoms
💊 HandiHaler capsules are for inhaling only. Never swallow them
😮 Common side effects: dry mouth, constipation, cough and sore throat
🩺 Get help for wheezing that gets worse straight after using it, eye pain or seeing halos, or if you cannot pee

Read about tiotropium: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. If you are very breathless, call 999.
