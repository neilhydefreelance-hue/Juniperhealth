Olanzapine: what to know 💊

🧠 An antipsychotic for schizophrenia and mania in bipolar disorder
🍽️ Common side effects: weight gain, a bigger appetite, sleepiness, dizziness, dry mouth and constipation
🚬 Smoking lowers olanzapine levels. Tell your team if you start or stop smoking
🚨 Call 999 for a high temperature with stiff muscles and confusion
🛑 Never stop suddenly. Your team will lower the dose slowly

Read about olanzapine: link in bio, juniperhealth.info

General information, not medical advice. Your weight, blood sugar and cholesterol will be checked regularly. If you are struggling, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
