Buprenorphine: what to know 💊

🧠 Used for opioid dependence, and as patches or tablets for pain. It eases withdrawal and cravings without the same high as heroin
😣 Common side effects: constipation, feeling sick, headache, sweating and sleepiness
🚨 Call 999 for slow or shallow breathing, or if someone is very sleepy and hard to wake
🚫 Never mix it with alcohol, benzodiazepines or other sedatives without medical advice. It can stop your breathing
💉 Ask your treatment service about naloxone, which can reverse an opioid overdose. Keep buprenorphine away from children
