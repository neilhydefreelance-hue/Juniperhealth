Tamsulosin: enlarged prostate medicine 💊

🚹 It relaxes the prostate and bladder neck so urine flows more easily, helping with a weak flow and needing to pee often
😵 Common side effects: dizziness when standing up, ejaculation problems, a blocked or runny nose and headache
🪑 Get up slowly from sitting or lying down
👁️ Having cataract surgery? Tell the eye surgeon you take tamsulosin
🩺 Get help if you faint, have an erection lasting more than 4 hours, or a severe skin rash
