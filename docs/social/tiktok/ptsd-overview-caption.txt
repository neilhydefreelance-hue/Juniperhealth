What is PTSD? Explained simply 💜

🧠 Around 1 in 3 people who have a traumatic experience are affected by PTSD. It can start years later
🌙 Symptoms include flashbacks, nightmares, avoiding reminders, feeling on edge, guilt and feeling numb
🔁 Complex PTSD can develop after repeated or long-lasting trauma
💬 Trauma-focused CBT and EMDR work for many people
🎖️ Veterans can contact Op COURAGE in England or Veterans' NHS Wales
