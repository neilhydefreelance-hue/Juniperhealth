Topiramate: what to know 💊

🧠 It treats epilepsy and is used to prevent migraines in adults
🖐️ Common side effects: tingling hands and feet, tiredness, less appetite, trouble finding words and low mood
🤰 It can harm an unborn baby. If you could get pregnant, you must follow a pregnancy prevention programme and use highly effective contraception
💧 It can make hormonal contraception less effective. Drink plenty of water
🚨 Get help straight away for sudden eye pain or blurred vision, not sweating and getting very hot, or side pain or blood in your pee

General information, not medical advice. Do not stop suddenly, as it can cause seizures. If you have thoughts of harming yourself, call Samaritans free on 116 123.
