Disabled Students' Allowance explained 💜

🎓 DSA gives extra help to university students with a disability, long-term condition, mental health condition or a learning difficulty like dyslexia or ADHD
💷 It is not means-tested and does not affect other student finance
💻 It can pay for assistive technology, note-takers, mentors, study skills tutors and extra travel
📝 Apply through Student Finance England or Wales, send evidence, then book a needs assessment
🏫 Talk to your university's disability service about adjustments like extra exam time
