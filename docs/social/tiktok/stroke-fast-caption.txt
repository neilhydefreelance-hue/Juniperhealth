Spot a stroke: think FAST 💜

😐 Face: has it dropped on one side?
💪 Arms: can they lift both and keep them there?
🗣️ Speech: is it slurred or muddled?
⏱️ Time: call 999 straight away
⚠️ Other sudden signs: loss of sight, weakness or numbness, dizziness, loss of balance or a severe headache
🚨 A mini stroke (TIA) is still an emergency. Call 999 even if symptoms go away
