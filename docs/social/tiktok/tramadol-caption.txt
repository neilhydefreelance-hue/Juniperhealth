Tramadol: what to know 💊

🩹 An opioid painkiller for moderate to severe pain, such as after surgery or injury. It works best for short-term pain
⚠️ It is a controlled drug and addictive. It is not recommended for most long-term pain
🤢 Common side effects: feeling sick, constipation, feeling dizzy or sleepy, headache and sweating
🚫 Do not mix with alcohol, benzodiazepines or other sedatives. It interacts with many antidepressants
🚑 Call 999 if someone has slow or shallow breathing, or is very sleepy and hard to wake
