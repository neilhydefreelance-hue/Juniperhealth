What is IBS? Explained simply 💜

🫃 Irritable bowel syndrome is thought to affect around 1 in 10 people in the UK
😣 Symptoms include tummy pain, bloating, diarrhoea, constipation and tiredness
☕ Triggers can include alcohol, caffeine, fizzy drinks, fatty or spicy food, stress and periods
🌿 Soluble fibre, peppermint oil, antispasmodics and talking therapies can help
🩺 See a GP for bleeding from your bottom, weight loss without trying, a lump, or a lasting change in bowel habits
