## Acid reflux (GORD)

```
💜 A space for people living with acid reflux (GORD), and those who support them. Share tips, ask questions and support each other. Acid reflux is when stomach acid comes back up into your food pipe, causing heartburn. When it happens often, it is called GORD. Our free guide: https://juniperhealth.info/conditions/digestive-and-liver/acid-reflux/ Peer support, not medical advice.
```
